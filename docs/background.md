Project Context and Scientific Synthesis

The Societal Inflammatory Simulator is a high-performance modeling environment designed to bridge the gap between individual biological stress and large-scale societal dysfunction. The project synthesizes four foundational scientific pillars into a single, unified state-engine:

The Multiscale Inflammatory Map (Vodovotz et al.): Provides the framework where individual stressors scale through neural and social networks to create "systemic inflammation," eventually manifesting as societal unrest or environmental impact.

Bowen Family Systems Theory: Defines the emotional field through the tension between Togetherness (TX) and Individuality (C). It treats the family as a "superorganism" that can either buffer or amplify anxiety.

Sapolsky’s Baboon Research: Establishes the link between social hierarchy/stability and physiological recovery. It provides the basis for our "Working on Self" recovery logic and the impact of chronic stress on basal regulation.

Ben-Jacob’s Bacterial Intelligence: Informs the "Collective Demand" and resource-pooling logic, where clusters reorganize and redistribute metabolic resources (M) to ensure the survival of the collective under extreme environmental pressure.
