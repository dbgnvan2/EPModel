# KB14 — The Best of Family Therapy

**Cite `KB14`. ASR source — no verbatim quotation.**

## K14.1 — The unpublished profile, quantified `[#]`

The ideal defined in **1961–62**: "some **20 variables**… in addition to about another **seven or eight** which are derivatives of that original 20."

The core of it: "human functioning is at its best when any person is **free to verbalize feelings, inner thoughts, and fantasies** to another person, and the other person is free to do the same **without either one reacting**, and without the fear that any verbalization would hurt the other."

He calls the simplified version *complete openness*, "achieved a few times in a lifetime, and it **quickly goes underground** for all people. It's an **idealized state**."

*Model consequence:* this is the profile `_KERR_INTERVIEWS.md` records as deliberately never published, now with a size. **Note the tension with `KB08` · K08.4**, where maintaining a self *requires* something not communicated. The reconciliation is probably that complete openness is the unattainable limit and reserve is the practical requirement — but it is a tension in the source and should be recorded, not smoothed.

## ⚠ K14.2 — Spouse selection is assortative on differentiation `[T]`

> "people choose spouses [at] **almost identical levels of differentiation of self**" — offered as "a fact that came from watching families back in research days", and "par for the course."

*Model consequence:* **a construction rule the spec does not state.** `M2.A`'s reference family happens to satisfy it (Ravi 38 / Marta 40), but nothing requires it. Any generated or edited instance must pair spouses at comparable `basic_level`, and this is testable.

## K14.3 — The relationship breaks at the ceremony, and he cannot explain it `[X]`

> "it is par for the course for people to have pretty good **friendship relationships before marriage** and then for the whole thing to get **messed up as of the time of the marriage ceremony**. I've often **wondered the why of that**, but there it exists as a fact."

*Model consequence:* an observed regularity with no mechanism offered — recorded honestly as such. It is adjacent to `L05.3`'s immediate pole assignment, which may be the same phenomenon seen from a different angle. **Flag for pass 2.**

## K14.4 — A graded scale of external-agent self-disclosure `[M]`

Three professional positions, ordered by how much self the agent puts in:

| Position | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Analyst** | encourages the other to communicate everything; **communicates no self back**; "the analyst is that neutral person who can listen to anything without having an opinion about it" |
| **Therapist** | "a little bit more real… a little bit more free to communicate self", which "**adds complications**" |
| **Counselor** | "communicates ideas… a way of thinking, a way of being — and in so doing, he carries out **the exact kind of thing that the parent does to the infant**. He instructs them in the ways of the world." |

*Model consequence:* `M1.E` has one external-agent type with a restricted repertoire. **This is a graded axis** — self-disclosure — with the counselor's instruction explicitly equated to parental programming of an infant. That is the same operation as the family projection process, performed by a professional, and the model should be able to represent it.

## K14.5 — Three (four) states where the ideal appears `[M]`

The infant–caretaker relationship, which he calls a **symbiosis** and stresses is a **normal state, not a pathology**; the marriage, "more in fantasy than in reality", best in courtship; the ideal analytic relationship; and a fourth — the charismatic leader, religion, "all kinds of charismatic things."

*Model consequence:* the model's fusion is uniformly a problem. **The infant–caretaker case is stated as normal**, which means fusion must be life-stage dependent rather than always costly.

## K14.6 — Sex as a contaminating variable `[T]`

A **person-to-person relationship** is friendship established *before* sex; "**sex is a binding and contaminating variable** that gets into these human relationships between two adults", and is not present between child and adult. Trial marriage is "living together **plus sex, always plus sex**."

*Model consequence:* the model's tie types do not distinguish this. Recorded as a stated property of adult ties, `[T]` but unquantified.

## K14.7 — Why "the Bowen Theory" `[T]`

He used *family systems theory* for about fifteen years and shifted to *the Bowen Theory* only because "systems" had become "almost a **social badge**" and was "misused so much that it's inaccurate" — needing a term that signalled a different meaning. He states his reservations plainly: using a proper name "**personalises it and makes it more vulnerable to becoming a dogma**", and that the change "may have increased the problem." → `KB09` · K09.1.

## K14.8 — Divorce figures `[#]`

About **50%** of first marriages ending in divorce, with second and third marriages worse, and rising. "It's the people with **lower levels of differentiation** who get caught up in this."

*Model consequence:* a population-level figure and a stated direction. Usable as a **Phase E** calibration target only — it is a societal statistic, not a per-family parameter.
