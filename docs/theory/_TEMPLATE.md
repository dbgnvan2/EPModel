# STREAM A — How families function

## A1. Phenomena requiring explanation
Observations Bowen reports that the theory had to account for. The *facts*, not the interpretation.
Each as: what was observed → in what population/setting.

## A2. Mechanisms proposed
How Bowen says it works. The causal machinery.
Each as: mechanism name → what it does → what it acts on.

## A3. Predictive claims
Statements of the form "X leads to Y", "if X then Y is more likely", "X predicts Y".
These are the falsifiable core. State each as a conditional. Flag whether Bowen offers evidence or asserts it.

## A4. Quantities, thresholds, time courses
Anything numeric or ordinal Bowen actually names: scale points, percentages, durations,
counts, "takes years", "within weeks", ranked orderings. Verbatim numbers only — do not infer.
Write "none in this chapter" if none.

## A5. Terminology
Terms introduced, defined, renamed, or used differently here. For each: term → Bowen's meaning
*in this chapter*. Note if a term seems to be doing a job a different term does elsewhere.

## A6. Structure and roles
Anything about who occupies which position: sibling position, parental roles, the
symptom-bearer, generational position, the person who moves first.

# STREAM B — How to do therapy  (→ the coaching agent)

## B1. What the therapist does
Concrete actions, in Bowen's own operational terms. Each as an action the agent could take.

## B2. What the therapist must NOT do
Prohibitions. Usually the more informative half.

## B3. Sequencing and preconditions
Order of operations; what must be true before a move is available; how long things take.

## B4. Stated failure modes
What Bowen says goes wrong, and what he says it causes. These are predictions in disguise.

## B5. What the technique assumes about the family
Every rule of technique implies a claim about how families work. Make each implied claim explicit.
This is the highest-value section — it converts prescription back into testable mechanism.
