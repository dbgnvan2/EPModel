## ⭐⭐⭐ FE11.1 — **The master theory was a prediction engine, and this project is its mechanisation** `[B]` `[T]`

> "The concepts about emotional systems and the differentiation of self were used to **make predictions
> about every item of abnormal behavior that might occur, and the therapeutic action that might change
> it. It was written in detail as the master theory. If the prediction was inaccurate, it meant that
> the theory was incomplete or there had been an error in the staff.** Errors by the staff or the
> therapists were relatively easy to change. **If the theory was in error, it meant the theory had to be
> modified or extended.** In the beginning there were quite a few errors. As time passed, there were
> fewer errors… **It was an impersonal 'rule book' that was always current, that 'knew everything' about
> misbehavior, ward management, and psychotherapy, that was communicated to the families ahead of
> time, and that prevented staff action based on angry feelings.**"

> "**A theory can be changed at any time by the logical thinking of respected people, but never in
> response to PERSONAL feelings of the moment.** The research theory was similar to 'the laws of the
> land.'"

And the same device in the therapy: "**The theory was always first. It governed everything that
occurred. When something occurred in the therapy that had not been predicted by the theory, the error
was either in the theory or in the therapist.**"

*Model consequence:* **the single most important framing finding in the whole run.**

The project has been treating the simulation as a novel artefact that must be defended against
`M11.F.3`'s general-systems objection. `FE11.1` shows that **Bowen built and ran exactly this
device**: an impersonal, exhaustive, always-current rule book that predicted every behaviour and every
intervention in advance, whose failures forced revision of the theory rather than of the record.

That is what `docs/bowen_agent_model_spec_v2.md` is. The model is not an imposition on the theory; it
is **the master theory in a form that can be executed**, and its acceptance criteria (`M11`) are
`FE11.1`'s error rule made mechanical: *a failed prediction means the theory is incomplete or the
implementation is wrong.*

**`M11.F` requirement candidate, and it should quote this passage.** It changes the honest description
of the project from "a general-systems artefact built over a natural-systems theory" to "a
computational instance of a device the theory's author designed, used for five years, and described
as central to the research." The general-systems objection still applies to the *implementation
choices*; it no longer applies to the *enterprise*.

Bowen also names the failure mode the device exists to prevent — "**prevented staff action based on
angry feelings**" — which is `M3.D.4`'s determinism requirement stated as its original purpose.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FE11.2 — The general-systems objection, **verbatim, in Bowen's own writing** — and it is not a prohibition `[B]` `[T]`

Three passages, and together they are far more precise than anything the spec currently cites.

> "In the 1940s, there were at least two kinds of systems theory already in existence. One was
> **general systems theory applied to the human by von Bertalanffy. It contained concepts from
> mathematics**… Another systems theory had been developed to deal with the **new technological advances
> in radar and the early computers.** Norbert Wiener, a professor of mathematics in Boston, had dealt
> more with technology. **Existing systems theory did not fit well with the idea of the human as an
> evolutionary being. To have used mathematical ideas would have further blurred the long quest toward
> science. To get beyond mathematics and technology, I fashioned a natural systems theory, designed to
> fit precisely with the principles of evolution and the human as an evolutionary being.**"

> "**To connect living things with the universe would require a very broad version of systems theory
> which did not participate in the questionable area of mathematics and physics. I am no longer
> prepared to debate all the issues that go into connecting living matter with a technological
> extension of the brain. Anyone is welcome to that field if they wish.** I chose to avoid that
> complexity by developing a natural systems theory based on the multiple variations of evolution."

> "**It is factual that certain aspects of human relationships can be described by any form of systems
> thinking, just as any mechanical machine can be a set of systems within systems. The analogy breaks
> down in the human when each family member has a brain that can control its part in the drama, and
> each is capable of thinking, voluntary action, reproduction, and a host of other functions.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M11.F.3` must be rewritten around these three passages, and the third one
justifies the pivot.**

1. **The objection is now quotable from Bowen.** `M11.F.3` currently cites `kb13`/`kb09` — ASR, under
   a no-verbatim constraint — and `M11.F.3a` rests on a `[user]` addition. Both can now quote the
   author directly. `FE00.1` and `FE01.14` give Kerr's version; this is Bowen's, and they agree.

2. **"Anyone is welcome to that field if they wish" is a declination, not a prohibition.** Bowen
   says he **chose to avoid** the complexity of connecting living matter to "a technological extension
   of the brain" — a computer — and explicitly leaves the field open. `M11.F.3` presents the
   objection as a standing charge against the model; it is more accurately a road Bowen declined to
   take, in 1988, while declining to rule on it. **That must be stated accurately and not softened in
   either direction.**

3. **The third passage names the exact defect the agent model was built to fix.** The
   mechanical-systems analogy "**breaks down in the human when each family member has a brain that
   can control its part in the drama**". A lattice of cells updated by a global rule is precisely the
   architecture that fails this test. A model of **autonomous agents, each perceiving, appraising and
   selecting its own move** (`M3.D.1`, `M4.D`) is the architecture that answers it.

   `CLAUDE.md` records the pivot from the grid engine as a design judgement. `FE11.2` shows it was
   **the correction Bowen names**, from the author, in the source's own terms. That belongs in the
   proposal and in `M11.F.3`.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FE11.3 — Bowen's own correction of "**projection**": it is **mutual description-transfer** `[B]` `[T]`

> "A decision was made to try and preserve the material for some future generation of research
> workers. It was a decision to **avoid coining new terms**, to use simple descriptive words when
> possible, and to make biological comparisons when appropriate. The term '**differentiation**' was
> similar to the differentiation of cells in embryology and biology. The terms '**fusion**' and
> '**cutoff**' describe the ways cells agglutinate and the way they separate to start new colonies of
> cells… Some terms were more difficult. **There was not a simple substitute for the 'projection
> process.' The term was well-known in psychology but inaccurate in biology. The process is really one
> in which qualities are transferred in the process of each describing the other.**"

> "**The term 'triangles' was most difficult**… The basic meaning of triangle is **lost when it is
> associated with geometry.**"

*Model consequence:* **Bowen calls his own term inaccurate and supplies the correct mechanism, which
is the one `FE07.4` describes and which the spec half-implements.**

*Qualities transferred in the process of **each describing the other***. Three consequences:

- **It is mutual.** `FE07`'s footnote 22 says the same from Kerr — "most accurately conceptualized as
  a **mutual** projection process" — and the two authors wrote independently (`FE11.0`). Two
  independent witnesses, so this is not one author's gloss.
- **The medium is description.** The transfer runs through **what each says the other is**, which is
  exactly `FE07.4`'s five-step loop (step 3: the mother treats the child as if her image of him is
  reality) and exactly what `M9`'s belief store holds. So `M9` is not a peripheral readout — it is
  **the channel the family projection process runs on**. That is the strongest argument yet for
  `FE02.7`'s missing belief→appraisal edge.
- **`M7.E.1c`'s name should carry the correction.** "Projection" implies a one-way emission from
  parent to child; the model's implementation must be a bidirectional description exchange, and the
  spec should say so where it uses the inherited term.

The terminology principle is worth recording for the project's own use: **avoid coining, prefer
plain descriptive words, make biological comparisons where they hold.** And the triangle warning is
pointed at exactly the reading a simulation invites — `M1.C`'s `Triangle` is not a geometric object.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FE11.4 — The three-child pattern, and the **"extra" child is the one who rises** `[B]` `[T]`

> "The immaturity is **governed by the parents rather than by the reality needs of the infants. It is a
> fairly fixed amount**, and can be played out with the one infant most emotionally involved with the
> parents… **This child is more dependent on the parents and becomes less differentiated than the
> immature parents. Other children emerge with about the same level of differentiation of self as the
> parents.** If the parental immaturity is great, it might involve a second child."

> "**Another infant grows up outside the emotional process between parents and children. It is commonly
> an 'extra,' perhaps the product of an unplanned pregnancy, whose basic emotional needs are met
> without having to live out a special role assigned by parental immaturity. These children grow up
> with slightly better levels of differentiation of self than the parents.**"

> "**The infant also plays a part. He/she can demand additional parental attention, and the immature
> parents comply.**" … "**It is determined solely by the way the parents create different levels of self
> in their children.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M7.E.1a`'s three-outcome distribution finally acquires the rule that says
*which* child gets which outcome.**

The spec requires a three-outcome distribution centred on the parental level with "the child's own
parental triangle deciding which offset" (`M7.E.1b`). `FE11.4` names the three positions and the
identifying feature of each:

| Outcome | Position | Identifying feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Lower** | most emotionally involved with the parents | carries the "fairly fixed amount" |
| **Same** | ordinary involvement | the default |
| **Higher** | **outside the emotional process** | often an **"extra"** — unplanned; needs met **without a role assigned** |

The upward branch is the one `M11.C.6` most needs and had least support for, and here it is with a
named mechanism: **a child to whom no role was assigned.** That is directly implementable —
`M7.E.1c`'s five projection-target situations select downward; the complement selects upward — and
it means the model produces both directions from **one** allocation rule rather than two.

"**a fairly fixed amount**" is `M1.D.1`'s `undifferentiation_budget` in Bowen's own words, and
"**governed by the parents rather than by the reality needs of the infants**" is the distinction
`FE07`'s reality-needs passage turns on.

Note the reciprocity clause: the infant "**can demand additional parental attention, and the immature
parents comply**" — so the target is not passively selected, which is `FE07.4`'s step 4 seen from the
child's side.

## ⭐⭐ FE11.5 — Two NIMH experiments: **functional level moved, basic level did not** `[B]` `[T]`

**The neutral-language experiment.** The staff worked for years to develop "**a neutral language that
did not categorize anyone. That was the most difficult task of all**… **The staff reached a point at
which they had to think before they spoke.**" The outcome:

> "**The familiar 'acting-out' almost completely disappeared and the ward became as quiet and orderly as
> any open ward. The basic impairment known as 'schizophrenia' did not change, but the patients were
> quiet and thoughtful.**"

**The responsibility experiment.** Parents were made responsible for the patient outside the ward;
public misbehaviour produced a stream of telephone calls; the parents "**routinely believed they had
been in control**".

> "Finally the parents were told that **if the calls did not stop, they would be replaced by research
> families who could manage psychotic misbehavior in public places. They knew the decision would be
> made solely on the presence of telephone calls. Not a single family was ever replaced. The calls
> stopped immediately. The parents went from being irresponsible to becoming super responsible.**"

> "**It suggested that the symptom complex known as schizophrenia is a manifestation of a faulty level of
> 'differentiation of self' in the human environment, rather than a disorder confined to schizophrenia
> alone.**"

*Model consequence:* **two clean natural experiments, and the first is the sharpest demonstration in
the corpus of `M1.A.5`'s basic/functional split.**

- **Change the environment's language → symptom expression collapses; the impairment does not
  change.** That is `functional_level` moving with `basic_level` held, observed by intervention
  rather than inferred. It is also `M9.3`'s belief-writing run in reverse: the *institution* stopped
  certifying the defect, and `M1.E.4`'s hysteresis released. **A ready `M11` fixture**, and one of
  the very few in the corpus where the manipulated variable is unambiguous.
- **Relocate responsibility → it transfers immediately and completely.** `M1.E.3` makes responsibility
  a conserved transferable quantity the family cannot hold while the agent holds it. `FE11.5` is that
  quantity moved by a **single structural change**, with an immediate and total effect and **no
  instance of the sanction ever being applied**. The threat's credibility was the whole mechanism —
  which is `M5.E.1`'s third rung ("if you do not, these are the consequences") used constructively,
  and `M1.A.15`'s dependence gate seen from the enforcing side.

Bowen's conclusion is `M1.D.4a` and `M11.C.7` together: the symptom is a property of the
**environment's** differentiation, so changing the staff's level changed the ward.

## ⭐⭐ FE11.6 — Leadership **relays** — and it settles the explainer's weakest convergence `[B]` `[T]`

> "It was a leaderless group. Passive decisions were made to '**get along with the others.**' Potential
> differences surfaced in the form of chronic emotional and physical illness. Family members began a
> constant process of blaming each other. **Finally, a more differentiated leader began to emerge.
> Instead of blaming the other person, he focused on himself. He lived his own life by example, instead
> of blaming. He became important to everyone in the relationship system. The family symptoms settled
> down. After several weeks, the leader's wife began the same slow process of becoming more sure of
> herself. The family slowly moved to a slightly higher level of differentiation.**"

> "**The family is a different organism when a leader emerges spontaneously from within the family.** The
> therapist may serve as a model for others, but **he is careful not to tell others what to do, or even
> to make suggestions. If he gets in the position of instructing family members, they will become
> dependent on the therapist, and the chronic cycle will continue. The differentiation pattern is one
> in which leadership shifts back and forth, until the entire family is safely out of the danger zone
> of undifferentiation.**"

*Model consequence:* **a shape claim for `M11.C.17` that the spec does not have, and a resolution for
`model_explainer.md` §16's weakest convergence.**

§16 records *the peripheral member moves first* as **weak, one setting, two series, and contradicted
by Ch05**, with a provisional reconciliation: the leader drives, the peripheral member performs the
visible change. `FE11.6` gives a third and better account in Bowen's own words: **leadership shifts
back and forth.** It is a **relay**, so both observations are of the same process sampled at
different moments, and neither "leaders first" nor "peripheral first" is the rule.

Three implementable consequences:

- **`M11.C.17`'s arm should assert a relay, not a single mover** — after one agent's `functional_level`
  rises and holds, a *second* agent's should follow, and the sequence should alternate rather than
  concentrate.
- **`[#]`: "after several weeks"** for the second mover — a usable lag for `M5.E.9`'s timescale table,
  which currently has ~1 week (acute reaction), months (re-equilibration) and years (a primary tie).
- **The coach constraint is a prohibition:** not telling others what to do **or even making
  suggestions**, because instruction produces dependence and sustains the cycle. `M1.E.2a` requires
  the objective to be *understand, not help*; `FE11.6` adds that even a suggestion is too much.
  That is stronger than `M5.B.5`'s repertoire restriction and should be stated.

## ⭐⭐ FE11.7 — **Power and punishment MUST NOT be modelled** `[B]` `[T]`

> "The leader of the group simply defines self to the others, ahead of time, following which the first
> person takes the expected action. **According to this approach, there is no such thing as one person
> taking action against another. The issue of 'power' or 'punishing' another person does not apply with
> the concept of differentiation of self. When people speak of family power, or punishing another, it
> simply means they do not understand the differentiation of self.**"

*Model consequence:* **an `M12` prohibition in Bowen's own words, and the spec has nothing like it.**

`M12.1` lists five mechanisms that must never be built, each withdrawn on the second reading of the
corpus. `FE11.7` supplies a sixth and it comes from the author directly:

> **`power` and `punishment` MUST NOT exist as mechanisms in the model.** No move may be represented
> as one agent acting *against* another; targets in `M1.F.1` are recipients, never victims.

This is not cosmetic. `M1.B.5`'s dominant pole, `M5.E.1`'s consequence rung and `M8.6`'s alignment
penalty could all be implemented as adversarial mechanics, and each would encode exactly the
misunderstanding Bowen names. The correct forms are already in the spec — the pole is a *reciprocal
allocation* (`M1.B.5`), the ladder is the *life-energy debit* surfacing (`M5.E.7`), alignment is a
*pseudo-self transaction* (`M8.6a`) — but nothing currently forbids the adversarial reading, and
`FE06.3`'s "he wins battles but loses wars" shows how naturally an observer falls into it.

The positive form is also implementable: **the leader defines self ahead of time, and the other then
takes the expected action.** That is `M5.D.2`'s `DEFINE` state with the anticipation made explicit,
and it is `FE07.5`'s "I will not" rather than "you will not".

## ⭐⭐ FE11.8 — **Less frequent contact worked better** — a measured reversal `[B]` `[#]`

> "**Until 1965, it was generally accepted that therapy families should be seen weekly. The families
> were seen three times a month by their regular therapists and once a month on my regular monthly
> visits. The families did poorly. I reduced the families to one family therapy meeting a month, and
> they did better than they would have done with weekly appointments. That was the beginning of a
> national trend toward less frequent appointments. It was possible when the therapist could avoid the
> impact of a transference relationship and the family used new knowledge to change itself.**"

> "**Over the years appointments have been reduced to biweekly, monthly, a few times a year, or even
> less. The frequency depends on the versatility of the family and the personal ability of the family
> to assume responsibility for itself.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M1.E.7e` — the coach tie must stay thin — with a *measured reversal* and a
dose range.**

Four sessions a month produced worse outcomes than one. That is `L11`'s "more contact ≠ more change"
with the sign made explicit and the crossover located, and the named mechanism is **transference
risk**, which in the model's terms is `M1.E.7e`'s accumulation of `investment` on the coach tie
turning the coach into a lender of pseudo-self (`FE04.15`).

Two requirements:

- **Coach contact frequency and outcome are non-monotone with a low optimum.** That is directly
  testable — vary only the coach's contact rate, hold everything else — and it is one of the few
  places the corpus gives an interventional dose response. **`M11.C` candidate.**
- **The optimum is family-dependent**: "the versatility of the family and the personal ability of the
  family to assume responsibility for itself." So the rate should be derived from the family's state,
  not fixed — which is `M10.A.2`'s preference and avoids an `[I]` constant.

Note the scope: this is the **coach** tie. `M5.B.3`'s prohibition on an optimum contact rate applies
to `REDUCE_CUTOFF` between *family members* and is untouched. The two are different ties and the spec
should say so explicitly, because the pair reads as a contradiction otherwise.

## ⭐⭐ FE11.9 — The February 1967 trip, from the mover's side — with the **preparation** named `[B]` `[T]` `[#]`

> "**Private letters went to one person in each important triangle. The goal was to cause the triangles
> to come to me, rather than my pursuing absent triangles. Not even my own nuclear family knew the
> plan. The key triangles knew the time of my arrival. My arrival on February 11, 1967, was a hallmark
> in the history of the family. Every important triangle in the family met in one living room. For some
> 12 years, I had been making regular trips home, with a little progress on each trip. I expected
> February 1967 to be a little better. By the time this new meeting was 30 minutes old, I knew that I
> was totally successful on the first try.**"

And what preceded it: after the six concepts were integrated in August 1966, "**When I awakened in
Tennessee there was a new awareness of triangles. Difficult triangles suddenly became obvious and
easy. The world was suddenly clear and structured.**"

*Model consequence:* **Ch21's trip from the other end, and it adds a phase the spec does not model.**

`M5.D` is a state machine that begins at `DEFINE`. `FE11.9` describes months of **preparation before
any move is emitted**: private letters to **one person in each important triangle**, timed so that
the triangles converge on the mover rather than the mover pursuing them. That is `M5.B.2`
`PREVENT_ALIGNMENT` and `M8.6b`'s routing used *in advance*, and it is the operational form of
"cause the triangles to come to me".

**Requirement candidate:** `M5.D` needs a `PREPARE` phase, or `M5.B.2` needs to be usable
pre-emptively on a whole triangle set rather than on one alignment.

Two `[#]` figures and one important shape claim:

- **12 years of prior trips**, months of planning, success recognised **within 30 minutes**. That
  bounds `M5.D.8` ("success MUST usually follow several failures") at the high end.
- **The discontinuity was in the frame, not in the attempts.** Twelve years of trying produced "a
  little progress on each trip"; the integration of the theory produced success "on the first try".
  That is `M1.E.7`'s frame supply in its purest instance — Bowen supplied his own — and it is the
  strongest available support for `M1.A.18`'s claim that the differentiating path is gated by
  perspective rather than by effort.

The ethics objection is recorded too, and it bears on `M8.8`: "**Some considered it unethical to do
something with the family without telling them ahead of time. They had the least knowledge about how
triangles are created.**"

## ⭐⭐ FE11.10 — The **two-thirds** treatability threshold `[B]` `[#]`

> "In general, **it was possible to find and establish a viable level of differentiation of self in
> everything above the upper two-thirds of those in the schizophrenic range. Below that it was not
> possible to find anyone in the family with a viable level of differentiation of self. Below the
> two-thirds level, it was as if the illness was already two or three generations deep, which was too
> fixed to modify anything except the current symptoms.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M1.D.4a` in Bowen's own writing, with a location.**

`M1.D.4a` requires a family-level `differentiation_capacity` — whether **any** member retains the
ability to take a position — and cites `kb08` · K08.2 (ASR). `FE11.10` states it in writing, adds a
threshold, and adds the *reason*: below it the process is already two or three generations deep.

That connects `M1.D.4a` to `FE08.1`'s quantum-jump conditions: the untreatable band is where the
downward conjunction has run for two or more generations, so **capacity and generational depth are
the same variable read two ways.** One quantity, and `M10.A.2` prefers it.

Bowen also gives the treatment that remains: "**a method of family group therapy for families in the
fixed range… as little as once or twice a year. With this approach, a sizeable number of patients
have been able to leave long-term custodial care in state hospitals and to live fairly comfortable
lives with their families at home.**" So below the threshold `functional_level` still moves —
`FE11.5`'s finding again — and only `basic_level` is fixed.

## ⭐ FE11.12 — The "**hearing**" model: reception is a three-band function of level `[B]` `[T]`

> "**In any audience, lay or professional, there is a small percentage that either 'hears' the presenter
> or asks pertinent questions. A much larger mid-scale group 'hears' part of the presentation and is
> motivated to hear more. They hear best through what others think rather than from within themselves.
> They can slowly learn to think for themselves rather than depend on others. The other percentage is
> the most fixed. They are prisoners of the emotional system, and teaching is slow and difficult. They
> hear very little and tend to be critical of the presenter, go to sleep, or otherwise absent
> themselves.**"

> "**It is harmonious with the concepts of 'differentiation of self' and 'societal regression.'**"

*Model consequence:* **`M1.E.7`'s landing rate as a population distribution, keyed to level.**

`M1.E.7` requires `systems_perspective` to rise only on a *landed* contact and sets the rate low
(`[#]` four or five useful contacts in two years, `K07.4`). `FE11.12` gives the shape across a
population: a small top band that receives directly, a large middle band that receives **through what
others think** — pseudo-self, `FE04.10` — and a bottom band that receives almost nothing and
responds with criticism or withdrawal.

That is `M1.A.18c`'s population requirement with a **reception** function attached, and it makes
`M11.C.17`'s coach arm richer: the same coach should land on a small minority, partially and
indirectly on the majority, and not at all at the bottom. The middle band's indirect route —
hearing through others — is also a **second-order** path the model has no representation for, and it
is how `FE11.6`'s relay would propagate.

## ⭐ FE11.13 — `systems_perspective` decays under **societal** pressure, not only tie load `[B]` `[T]`

> "**Trainees can develop a pretty good idea of family systems theory while they are in the program,
> which is quickly lost when they are exposed to the popular pressure from society in their home
> environments.** An effort is constantly being made to select trainees who can continue to believe
> what they believe, in spite of all the minute pressures… **We all have some kind of vulnerability to
> believe what we are supposed to believe, rather than what we ourselves believe.**"

> "Some of the residents did extremely well in family therapy **as long as they were supervised, but
> they quickly lost their theoretical orientation in their home cities of practice. Their newly
> acquired theory faded into the conventional theory of the place where they practiced.**"

*Model consequence:* **a decay term the spec does not have.**

`M4.C.4` makes `systems_perspective` fall with **acute anxiety**; `M1.A.18d` attenuates it per tie by
that tie's **emotional intensity**. Neither covers `FE11.13`: the perspective is lost to

## FE11.17 — Bowen's own account of the fourteen NIMH ideas `[B]` `[T]`

> involvement of the extended families, (11) **the multigenerational transmission process**… (12) **the
> therapist's involvement of self**… (13) the fact that these are all **systems components of the large
> emotional system**, which is the family, (14) **meshing of the family system with the environment**, to
> describe the ways the family is part of the total of society."

*Model consequence:* two of the fourteen are **not** among the eight published concepts (`FE10.16`)

- **(12) the therapist's involvement of self** — `M1.E.2`'s drifting reactive state and `M1.E.6`'s

- **(14) meshing of the family system with the environment** — `M1.D.6`'s `access_vector` and

places a reader might assume the project invented the requirement.

the new ideas for the long-term future of psychiatric research.**"
