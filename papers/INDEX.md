# Method-literature catalog — agent-based simulation of human behaviour

Raw PDFs live in this folder (gitignored); this catalog is the committed index.
**This is the *method* literature** — how to build computational models of human
behaviour with interacting agents. It is completely separate from the Bowen
family-systems corpus in `docs/theory/`.

Facet keys:
- `ARCH` — agent architecture for believable human behaviour (memory/planning/reflection, multi-agent social sim)
- `FIDELITY` — fidelity/validity of simulated populations (algorithmic fidelity, silicon samples)
- `TRAIT` — traits, persona, emotion in agents (persona vectors, emotion representations)
- `ADAPT` — adaptation / learning in agents (self-adapting, continual learning)
- `DYNAMICS` — emergent behaviour & safety (misalignment, cascade)
- `MECH` — mechanistic computational models of behaviour/psychiatric process (RL/TDRL)
