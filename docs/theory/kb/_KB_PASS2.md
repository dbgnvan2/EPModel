# Pass 2 — the interviews read against each other, the book and the lectures

Pass 1 extracted each interview on its own. This pass re-reads the fifteen against each other, against the 22 chapters and against the 1979 lectures, and resolves the eleven items carried out of pass 1.

**Method note.** Pass 1 over-read the book, and pass 2 caught it. The same risk applies here, so every high-stakes pass-1 claim was re-verified against the transcript before being carried forward. **Ten checked, nine confirmed verbatim, one mis-attributed in a checking script rather than in the document.** The corrections below are therefore mostly *scope* corrections rather than withdrawals — which is itself the pattern pass 2 predicted.

## ⛔ WITHDRAWN

### "Eight to ten generations" is not a calibration target — `L16.3`, `model_explainer.md` §8

**Verified verbatim** (`KB11`): "That was the place I introduced the notion of ten generations, or the creation of schizophrenia, **which I put in not to say that it takes ten generations**. Previously I'd said it took only three, but that was sort of **to convey the notion that it's not just three and just ten, but it is a multi-generational thing**."

The ledger records it as "revised **up** from at least three", implying a measurement refined. It was not refined; **both numbers were rhetorical devices for the same qualitative point.**

This is the third figure to fail the same way, after the per-generation decline rate (`L09.7`) and the annual societal-regression rate (`L13.1`). **The pattern is now established well enough to state as a rule: a number offered by Bowen in illustration is not a measurement, and the corpus contains no counter-example.**

**What survives:** the direction — transmission is multigenerational, more than three, unquantified.

## ✂ CORRECTED — scope, not substance

### The sinks: four absorb, three symptomise — both halves are needed

`KB04` verified: "The family has a whole spectrum of ways of **dealing with, absorbing, or managing, deactivating** the anxiety. There are **four principal ones**. It's **distance between the spouses**, marital conflict… sickness in the spouse… projection to children."

1979 Tape 2 verified: "keeping distance from each other is one of the better ways. I list **the three ways** they deal with it **because these are the three things that end up with symptoms**."

**They answer different questions and both are true.** Distance absorbs anxiety and does not produce a symptom. The spec (`M6.I.1`, `M6.I.2`) states only that distance sits outside the budget; it does not state that distance *absorbs*, which is why a family can discharge into it indefinitely and read as untroubled.

### The three societal variables sit above the three dials, not beside them

`KB10` verified: "the way the person thinks, another is differentiation of self, and a third is the **intensity of anxiety**. Those are **three main variables**."

The spec's `societal_leadership`, `media_amplification` and `resource_pressure` are **drivers of his third variable**, not competitors with his three. They are one level below. The spec presents them as the societal model, which overstates them.

**Resolution:** keep the dials as anxiety drivers; state Bowen's three as the layer above; note that *mode of thinking* has no representation in the model at all and is not currently implementable.

### General systems versus natural systems — both statements are his

`KB13`: general systems is not the answer; natural systems, rooted in the earth and in evolution, is. `KB09`: "I never really understood general systems and he never really understood what I meant by natural systems, but **the two are compatible within limits**."

**Neither is a retraction of the other.** The honest position: he preferred a foundation rooted in nature, regarded general systems as a different thing that came "out of man's head", and thought the two overlapped without being the same. A simulation is a general-systems artifact, so this remains a real named objection — but "compatible within limits" is the softer and later-qualified form and must travel with it.

### The fractal justification is not Bowen's — three independent statements

- `KB02`: "an **analogy is not an extension of theory**"; sociobiology's inference from animals to humans is "a **quantum jump, which I would never have done**."
- `KB10`: on the triangle in society — "it's an analogy, but **not a reasonable connection. These things don't connect up.**"
- `KB13`: society goes through cycles "**similar to a family. Not the same as, but the same principles apply.**"

**Settled formulation:** *the same principles apply at different levels; the levels are not the same system; reasoning from one to another is analogy, not derivation.* The proposal's §7 justification for deferring the societal layer — that Bowen is "explicitly fractal" — must be replaced with this.

### Technique is family-type dependent — and the corpus gives both techniques

`KB05`/`KB06`: for a **peace-agree** family, devise an old issue to raise workable reactivity. For a **reactive, explosive** family, the opposite — "double time on calming one's own emotional reactiveness… **just be less reactive than the others**."

`KB08` supplies the cost of getting this wrong, with three outcomes: acute psychosis, a self-blinding, a suicide. **This upgrades the family-type gate from an efficiency parameter to a safety property.**

## ➕ ADDED BY PASS 2 — cross-source findings no single interview carries

### The "asserting it is evidence against it" rule is now five-source and can be stated generally

| Source | Form |
|---|---|
| `KB04` | "I'm not going to get into your problem" — "**that in itself is an indicator of involvement**" |
| `KB03` | "I'm out of it, you handle it" — "**that is mostly denial, because they are in it**" |
| `KB06` | "the more the individual has to say **'I've worked it out'**, is evidence of an attachment" |
| `KB12` | a low-level self holds a **selfish, dogmatic, forceful** I-position |
| `KB07` | catching oneself **diagnosing, criticising or praising** is a loss of systems perspective |

**Rule:** *any assertion of a differentiated, detached or resolved state is negative evidence for it.* Five independent forms across five interviews. This is the strongest cross-source pattern in the interview corpus and the model has no readout for it.

### The research attitude beats the therapeutic one — four statements, an experiment and a replication

`KB04`: research families did better than therapy families. `KB03`: three further statements, **an experiment** (residents told to learn rather than cure; the best lasted ten hours before the family demanded an answer and he gave in), and **an independent replication by Kerr** on cancer families, who produced more ideas per session and "were doing better, no question about it."

**This is now the best-evidenced claim about the coach anywhere in the project** — better evidenced than anything in the book.

### Three coach-state detectors, and the model has one

`L20.2` the urge to criticise · `KB04` the head inside the other's problem, with a **recovery move** and humour as the return signal · `KB07` diagnosing, criticising **or praising**.

The third is two-sided and the model's is negative-valence only. **Praise is the half that would be missed.**

### Openness has a floor as well as a ceiling

`KB08`: "to maintain a self, there has to be **some self that self does not communicate** to the other"; total disclosure is "a **de-selfing** kind of thing." `KB14`: complete openness is "an **idealized state**… achieved a few times in a lifetime." `KB05`: cutoff "is your **lifeline**", reducible only as fast as the person can absorb.

With `L12.4`'s two-sided closeness band, four sources agree: **contact is not monotonically good**, and both the model's `REDUCE_CUTOFF` and its open-relationship goal need floors.
