# KB06 — Defining a Self in One's Family of Origin, Part 2

**Citation rule:** cite `KB06`, not a speaker. ASR source — no verbatim quotation.

---

## K06.1 — Triangles are the blueprint, not the lever `[T]` — confirms `M1.C.3b` outright

> "Triangles are very important for **understanding it theoretically. I mean there's the blueprint.** But people tend to get too involved in looking for the triangle, trying to de-triangle self, and **I tend much more now to don't bother much with the triangles.** I just like to go **sit alongside the family and not react**, and when any person can do that the family can be calm."

*Model consequence:* the plainest statement in any source that triangles are an explanatory structure rather than an operating target. `M1.C.3b` stands as written. Note also the mechanism given in the same breath — one person not reacting is sufficient to calm the family — which is `L10.4`'s chain-reaction interrupt.

## K06.2 — The six principles, with their paired failure modes `[T]`

| # | Principle | The failure it names |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | An **individual, person-to-person relationship with each member** of the whole family | fusing into the group and letting the family define self |
| 2 | Work the **primary triangle**, and put the effort on **mother** first | relating to **both parents together** |
| 3 | Go home **frequently**, and especially when **anxiety is up** — sickness, death, wedding, christening, anniversary — getting individual time with each person present | fusing into the group and re-enacting the old role; drinking; acting out |
| 4 | Learn the language of emotion, systems and triangles; one's own family is the best place to learn | going home expecting to be told one is loved and approved of — "a **helpless child position**" |
| 5 | Accept that **family and parents did the best they knew how** at the time | blaming, which keeps self helpless and reactive |
| 6 | Go back **alone** | taking spouse and children — "excess baggage… you **prohibit individuality** when you do it" |

And an overarching one: **avoid confrontation** — "confrontation probably says more about the **immaturity of the accuser** than it does about the personal deficit in the accused."

## ⚠ K06.3 — Work mother first; the father relationship then arrives on its own `[T]`

> "I would put much more effort on **mother** than on father because he's out there and ready for a relationship **as soon as the person can resolve some of the intensity with mother**. Resolve some of the intensity with mother and the relationship with father is waiting and it **occurs automatically**."

The mechanism: parents preserve a oneness in which one becomes spokesman — "Mother was the mouth", and Dad "didn't speak as a person, but he spoke more as what mother wanted."

*Model consequence:* an **ordering constraint between ties** that the model does not have. It is not about who moves first (`C5`) but about which tie to work first, and it predicts the second relationship resolves without being worked. Testable as a two-arm criterion. Also note the corollary — **never relate to both parents together** — which is the person-to-person requirement, and is a *different* claim from Ch03's group-size finding (that one concerns a therapy session; this concerns establishing a relationship).

## ⚠ K06.4 — Two family types, two opposite techniques, and a relative criterion `[T]`

Confirms and completes `KB05` · K05.1. For a **peace-agree** family, whose differences "are obliterated quickly", he devised an old issue "as a device" to raise enough reactivity to work with. For a **reactive, explosive** family the technique inverts:

> "one puts **double time** on trying to calm one's own emotional reactiveness and to go in on the emotional scene and **be less reactive than the others**. You don't have to control your reactiveness all the way. **Just be less reactive than the others**, and you've made a step toward calming a family."

> "they take what I had described as a formula for my family and attempt to use it on all of them. **It doesn't work. There are different kinds of families.**"

*Model consequence:* two things. The `M5.C` live-issue gate **must** carry a family-type term. And the success criterion for a reactive family is **relative, not absolute** — less reactive than the others present — which is the same comparative form as Ch03's pole-flip rule (`L03.1`, self-assertion "greater than" the other's domination) and is directly implementable.

## K06.5 — Degree of cutoff estimates level of differentiation `[T]`

> "The **degree of cutoff gives a rough estimate of the level of differentiation** in self."

*Model consequence:* a readout that requires no self-report and no rater — the model can compute it from tie state. Given that every direct estimate of differentiation is biased by construction (`L17.2`), this is the most useful observable found so far, and it belongs in §10 alongside the three that lie.

## K06.6 — The family actively prevents one-to-one contact `[T]`

> "It's average for families to so work it that it makes it terribly difficult to get any one person alone for any length of time. **The family system is ingenious in preventing this**, in forcing you to relate to a group."

*Model consequence:* a system-level counter-move the model has no representation of. The family is not passive during a differentiating effort; it acts to restore group-relating. This is the togetherness force with an operational signature.

## K06.7 — Claiming to have resolved it is evidence of attachment `[T]`

> "that kind of a posture is evidence of an attachment, and probably **the more the individual has to say 'I've worked it out,' is evidence of an attachment**."

*Model consequence:* C10 again, in a third form — after "declaring detachment is evidence of involvement" (`KB04` · K04.10) and the low-level I-position (`KB12` · K12.3). The pattern is consistent enough to state generally: **asserting a differentiated state is negative evidence for it.**

## K06.8 — Go up the tree, not out to siblings `[T]`

> "one can spend **too much time on one's own siblings**. One is related to one's brothers and sisters because one had the same parents, but each one is different, and to really differentiate a self from a parent, you **go back up the family tree** instead of going out to one's own siblings."

And the maternal side yields more, "because of the way society is organized. Mother is the one who spends most of the time with the kids."

*Model consequence:* the differentiating move has a **preferred direction on the graph** — ancestral, and maternal-biased. The model treats all ties as equivalent for this purpose.

## K06.9 — Membership is by lived relationship, not biology `[T]`

For dead parents: go to "the **emotional field in which that parent lived**" — relatives, cousins, and non-kin. Cases given: a club the mother founded in high school whose members still meet; the mother's wedding bridesmaid, who "became a kind of a **functional mother**". "There's something **magical about contacting cousins**." People who say they have no family "find family that they never knew existed."

For adoption: work the adoptive family — the patterns are "almost identical", differing at most in intensity.

*Model consequence:* strong confirmation of `M1.A.12` and Ch07's `L07.1` — the emotional system is defined by involvement, not kinship. It also means the model's graph must admit **functional substitutes** for a missing member.

## K06.10 — Group and network formats give a short gain and then revert `[T]`

Bringing family members together to say what they think "is okay within limits. If handled well, there's a **short-term gain. At best, the gain lasts for a brief time, and then the family goes back** to where it was before… **At worst, this kind of an approach can drive a family apart, and it takes years to repair it.**" Continuing formats "make the family **dependent on the therapist**."

> "**differentiation is not possible when one keeps this up**… I'd rather have a little bit of differentiation and skip the togetherness for the years ahead than to have the pleasure of togetherness for a week."

*Model consequence:* C1 and `L10.1` confirmed from the family-of-origin side, with the same two-axis structure — togetherness rises, differentiation does not — and an explicit statement that the two trade against each other over the long run.

## K06.11 — A coach is needed, and its function is recognising the togetherness trap `[T]`

> "for the average person who's going to put time and energy into this **they need a coach** — someone who has already covered the ground and who **can be aware when the family gets snookered into the togetherness type thing**, which is self-defeating, and can **literally save them years**."

*Model consequence:* the coach's function is stated narrowly and it is not direction, interpretation or support. It is **detecting a specific failure mode**. That is implementable, and it fits `M11.C.7` — the coach is a position with a detector, not a source of change.

## K06.12 — Rigid application of the rules fails `[T]`

A husband applied the go-alone rule dogmatically, refusing to bring his wife and children when newfound relatives asked about them; "his newfound relatives **refused to see him anymore**." The comment: "at that point, I would have taken my wife and kids and taken them over and said, 'Here they are, look at them.'"

*Model consequence:* the rule governs **who does the work**, not whether the family may be seen. A model that implements the go-alone gate as an absolute reproduces the failure rather than the principle.

## K06.13 — Needing company is a readiness test `[T]`

> "people are afraid to go back to their own families, and they need a husband and a wife to hold their hands… **if you're not up to going back to seeing your own family by yourself, why don't you just wait until you get that kind of strength?**"

*Model consequence:* a self-administered readiness gate, and one more instance of the pattern that the state gates the move rather than the move creating the state.

## K06.14 — Rate of progress has three inputs `[T]`

> "The rate of progress depends on the **intensity of the problem in the family**, the **ability of the differentiating one to comprehend** the problem, and to become **effective in dealing with** his own family."

And a stated duration: someone who "after about **four years**, finally now I am able to listen to mother without hearing her as being critical" — corroborating `KB05` · K05.8 with a number.
