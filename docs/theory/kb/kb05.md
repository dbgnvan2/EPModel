# KB05 — Defining a Self in One's Family of Origin, Part 1

**Citation rule:** cite `KB05`, not a speaker. ASR source — no verbatim quotation. See `_SOURCE_QUALITY.md`.

---

## ⚠ K05.1 — Technique is family-type dependent, and the model treats it as universal

Explaining the anonymous paper's "tempest in a teapot": his own family is a **peace-agreed family** whose differences "are obliterated quickly", so he deliberately avoided a here-and-now issue and "carefully planned to **resurrect a very old issue**."

> "the people who've read that paper often get into going back with **confrontations**… for a lot of families that **stay stirred up, that is the exact opposite of what you do**, and people somehow miss that point, and they go back and stir up a family, which then rejects them."

*Model consequence:* **`M5.C`'s live-issue precondition is conditional on family type and the spec states it unconditionally.** Ch21's provocation was calibrated to a conflict-avoidant family; for a chronically conflicted one, provoking is the error. The gate needs a family-level term — provoke a peace-agreed family, do not provoke a stirred-up one — and `M11.C.5`'s arms should differ by family type.

The other named misconception is the opposite move: "the commonest is to go back and attempt a **love-in**, a togetherness thing… It doesn't respect the person who has to have distance from the family to be comfortable."

## ⚠ K05.2 — Cutoff is a lifeline, so reducing it has a rate limit

> "there couldn't be a cut-off unless **self plays a part in it**, and one just does not go around and stop cutting off from people because **cutting off from people is your lifeline which enables you to live and adjust**. So you can reduce your tendency to cut off **just as fast as you can**" — and no faster.

It is also bilateral: "if you're trying to relate back to a side of the family that is cut off from you and **doesn't care to see you**, then that's the two sides of the cut off."

*Model consequence:* `M5.B.3` currently takes Ch22's "as frequently as possible" and forbids an optimum rate. **KB05 adds a floor-side constraint the model lacks:** cutoff is load-bearing for the person maintaining it, so removing it faster than they can absorb is harmful, and the other side has its own state. `REDUCE_CUTOFF` needs a per-person rate limit and a bilateral gate, not just an absence of penalty.

## K05.3 — You cannot differentiate from your parents alone `[T]`

Years of working the two parental relationships individually: "I did pretty good, but I **still would get caught up in it** when I would go home."

> "one cannot differentiate a self from one's two parents alone. The parents are a part of a **gigantic emotional amalgam** that goes far into the past… how do you differentiate yourself from a parent who never differentiated herself from a grandparent who never differentiated herself?"

Multigenerational history is near-necessary: "I can't define how important it is, but it's important enough that **I don't think anybody makes it without doing it**."

*Model consequence:* the strongest statement yet for `M1.D.8` and `M2.3` — a household-closed model cannot support the differentiating move at all, not merely miscompute its driving term. Breadth of the family-of-origin graph is a **precondition**, not a refinement.

## K05.4 — Why gains leak: the partner reverts through ties the mover is not in `[M]`

> "I'd think I'd have things pretty clear with my mother… and I leave and very quickly **she's back where she was before**… what she would do then would **change in relationship to my other siblings**, based on what was going on between she and them."

*Model consequence:* the change-back reaction is mediated by the target's *other* ties, not only by the acting tie. `M5.E.5` has push-back surfacing as symptom in a third person; this adds the reverse channel — the gain decays through relationships the mover never touched. Directly explains why K05.3's breadth requirement exists.

## K05.5 — The operational simplification: one clump, watch for side-takers `[T]`

> "just put your entire family in a **conglomerate together and watch them all**. In other words, **instead of messing around with triangles**, just see that one gets oneself emotionally detached from everybody in it, and **watch out for the ones who want to come over on your side**."

*Model consequence:* independent confirmation of `M1.C.3b` — the coach does not work triangles — and of Ch21's clump-and-ally rule, here stated as the *simplification that replaced* triangle work. It also confirms `M8.6`: the thing to watch for is side-taking.

## K05.6 — Bridging a cutoff resurrects the original conflict `[M]` — a testable prediction

A cutoff originating in "a triangle with a mother with the negative side between two siblings" can persist a hundred years, with descendants unaware of each other. When someone re-establishes contact across it:

> "there will emerge in that family a pretty clear **replica of that conflict**… now these two clans have met, and that **stirs up a replica of the same conflict, anxiety**."

With a case: a trainee who approached the wrong clan in Ireland "wound up getting sick and vomiting in his hotel room"; the next day, with the right clan, "he was in a **love-in**."

*Model consequence:* `M4.A.3`'s `RECONCILIATION` converts standing load into interaction load. This says it does something sharper — it **instantiates a replica of the original conflict**, which is a specific, falsifiable prediction and a natural acceptance criterion.

## K05.7 — Withdrawn and effervescent are equally involved `[T]` — a readout warning

> "the **withdrawn, obsessive person has the same degree of problem as the effervescent, hysterical person**… Both equally emotional. Both equally involved."

And: "**You can be far away from your family and still be close, or you can live in it and be far away from it.**"

*Model consequence:* a third entry for `model_explainer.md` §10.1's readouts-that-lie — **apparent distance or calm is not evidence of differentiation.** It also corroborates C7 from a new angle: geography and coupling are orthogonal.

## K05.8 — Hearing criticism is evidence of your own reactivity `[T]`

> "Finally, I can be with my mother **without hearing her as being critical**. Well, if this person is **hearing** mother as being critical, then **they probably are being critical, defensive**."

*Model consequence:* a clean, cheap readout that couples the belief layer (`M9`) to reactivity — the receiver's *perception* of hostility is an observable of the receiver's own state, not of the sender's. The model has appraisal reading intensity and conductance; this makes perception itself diagnostic.

## K05.9 — Not therapy, and not diagnosis `[T]`

> "trying to be a therapist for your family, the whole thing is **useless**." The goal is "managing your own emotional reactivity while there, on the scene."

And a named obstacle that "can take people years": getting out of the diagnosing frame of reference. The commonest failure is going home as an expert — "people try to **cram it down their families' throats**."

*Model consequence:* the coach's objective again (`KB04` · K04.12), now applied to the *member* rather than the professional. Whatever the model's `I-POSITION` does, it must not be a knowledge-transfer or persuasion move.

## K05.10 — Timing, stated repeatedly `[#]`

"Not done in a weekend, and not in a therapist's office… for most of us, it's done over a period of **several years, if at all**." Bowen puts himself at nine years to get "a leg up", and is still working on it with his mother at 93.

*Model consequence:* corroborates `M5.D.7a` — the ratchet increment must be small — from the author's own account, twice.

## K05.11 — The discovery path, dated `[T]`

Triangles defined by ~1964; multigenerational histories from 1960–61; the family-of-origin trip August 1966; used in teaching within a month "with tremendous results… a **complete surprise**. I had absolutely no intention of using this for therapy"; by 1969 "pretty sure it was not a fluke that one can gain **more emotional maturity in one's family of origin than working in the nuclear family** and the here and now."

*Model consequence:* dates and confirms `L14.3`/`L22.4` — the extended family displaces the nuclear family as the site of work — and shows it was found accidentally, through teaching, not designed as therapy.
