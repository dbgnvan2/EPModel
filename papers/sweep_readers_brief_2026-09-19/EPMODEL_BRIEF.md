# EPModel brief for paper readers

EPModel is a simulation of emotional process in ONE three-generation family (about 12 agents), built on Bowen family systems theory. It is a consistency engine for one theory ("if Bowen's account is right, what follows for a family shaped like this?"), not a predictor of real families. Spec v2 is approved (427 requirements, 16 modules, 34 acceptance criteria). No code yet; the NEXT piece of work is the implementation plan, then Phase B build in a new Python package `src/bowen/`. An older NumPy grid engine (10,000 units on a 100x100 lattice, stress contagion field) is frozen and being replaced because it has no dyads and so no triangles.

## The v2 design (rule-based, NOT LLM-driven)

- Objects: `Person` (slow `basic_level` of differentiation, fast `functional_level`, chronic and acute anxiety, symptom load in 3 channels, life-energy budget, beliefs, inbox); `Relationship`/tie (undirected conductance, bond energy, directed functioning balance and investment, taboo set, per-edge latency, cutoff flag); `Triangle` (persistent topology stored apart from the currently active set; inside pair/outside member; positions shift under load); `Family` (undifferentiation budget with three sinks, societal anxiety, nodal-event calendar, shared beliefs, multigenerational ledger); external agents (coach, doctor, teacher) are full Persons with real ties, not services.
- Agents do not talk. They exchange typed `Event` records: sender, targets, witnesses, move type, intensity, timestamp, route, per-hop fidelity, duration, `exogenous` flag. Events pop in time order into inboxes; simultaneous events are batched so order within a tick cannot decide the outcome. Witnesses who overhear also appraise.
- Each fast tick (1 week) each person selects exactly ONE of nine moves (PURSUE, DISTANCE, CONFLICT, OVERFUNCTION, UNDERFUNCTION, TRIANGLE, CUTOFF, I-POSITION, STAY-IN-CONTACT) plus a WITHHOLD outcome. Policy has two channels: automatic (discharge anxiety now; learns = family style) and self-directed (hold a position through discomfort; does not learn). Mixing weight is a function of differentiation. Slow tick = 1 year (differentiation drift, chronic anxiety, life stage, mortality). Runs cover ~40 years (~2,080 fast ticks).
- Invariants: anxiety conserved and redirected, never destroyed; family undifferentiation budget with three sinks; dyadic exchange conserves pseudo-self; no exit from the field.
- A belief layer: what the family holds true is separate from what is true; agents act on beliefs; institutional acts write to it with hysteresis.
- Exogenous events arrive as spells with a duration (never per-tick coin flips). Illness and job loss are primarily OUTPUTS generated by accumulated load; national incidence statistics are a calibration target, never an event generator.

## Epistemic position (this matters for what lessons are useful)

- The source corpus supplies directions, orderings and mechanisms but almost NO magnitudes. Every constant is invented and labelled as such. There is no validation data: no instrument, no rated families, no numbers assigned to persons.
- Therefore the trusted output is the DIFFERENCE between two arms of a counterfactual, run over ensembles of seeds (N=12 gives no law of large numbers; never a single run). Acceptance tests assert a direction of difference between two arms, never an absolute threshold, and each test must be proved failing by mutation (delete the mechanism, confirm the test goes red).
- No counterfactual may be reported from parameters tuned to reproduce a known history (fitting breaks independence; non-identifiability). Initial conditions are not nuisance parameters; imported families run as ranges and outputs are envelopes.
- Engine must be pure: no UI, no file I/O; it emits log records through a caller-supplied interface; logging must be a pure observer (same seed, same final state with logging on or off). A run log plus a deterministic readable-trace renderer is specified (module M16).
- Known readout traps: overt emotionality peaks mid-scale, position-taking can be counterfeit, any estimate of differentiation is biased by borrowed pseudo-self.

## A second, exploratory line: LLM-driven agents

The owner has separately explored whether LLM agents could act like humans in maturity and emotional reactivity (e.g. "a 15-year-old brat", "a shy 10-year-old"), using persona prompting, steering/persona vectors, memory, and whether an agent could change its own maturity over time. Open problems he identified: timescale (decades of development), what "more mature" means operationally, validation, and how goal conflict and learning would work in a small system of interacting agents with stressors.

## What I need from each paper

For each paper, report only what the paper actually says (cite section/page/figure/table; quotes must be under 15 words). Separate clearly: (a) what the paper states or shows, (b) your own inference about relevance to EPModel. If a paper has little or nothing transferable to a 12-agent theory-driven family model, say so plainly; do not stretch.

Look specifically for:
1. Simulation formalism: state representation, event/scheduling design, synchronous vs asynchronous update, handling of simultaneous events, time scales, determinism and seeding.
2. Agent architecture: decision rule/policy, memory, reflection, perception, heterogeneity, bounded rationality, learning/adaptation.
3. Interaction/network structure: dyads, groups, influence mechanisms, thresholds, cascades, tie dynamics, information distortion.
4. Initialization: how agent attributes and initial conditions are set; sensitivity to them.
5. Calibration and validation: how they validated, against what data, at what level (micro/macro), what validation they could NOT do, sensitivity analysis, robustness across seeds/models/prompts, variance reporting, ablations.
6. Reported failure modes, biases, limitations, negative results (especially for LLM agents: prompt sensitivity, model dependence, homogenization, drift, sycophancy/consensus bias, cost).
7. Software engineering lessons: modularity, logging/observability, reproducibility, experiment configuration, intervention/counterfactual tooling.
8. Anything about small-N systems, emotional states, family/household units, stress, or long-horizon (years/decades) dynamics.
