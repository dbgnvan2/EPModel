## ⭐⭐⭐ FE04.1 — The pseudo-self transfer, worked, with numbers that **conserve exactly** `[K]` `[#]`

> "For example, **two people with basic levels of 35 who marry might, during the course of the
> marriage, do enough 'borrowing' and 'trading' of 'selves' that one spouse's functional level rises
> to an average of 55 and the other's drops to an average of 15.**"

Footnote 9 supplies the observable side:

> "In the 55–15 discrepancy in functional levels described above, **the one at 15 may be alcoholic
> and housebound with severe phobias. The one at 55 may have a very responsible job and be in
> excellent health.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M6.I.4`'s conservation is stated numerically, and it balances.**

```
before:  35 + 35 = 70
after:   55 + 15 = 70        transfer = 20, exactly antisymmetric
```

This is the **only arithmetic instance of the invariant anywhere in the corpus** (the papers' only
arithmetic is Ch09's two-level functioning). Three consequences:

1. **`M6.I.4` gains a `[#]` fixture.** A pair at matched `basic_level` 35, run to divergence, must
   land at functional levels summing to twice the basic level within the stated tolerance
   (`M6.1`). That is a directly assertable invariant test, not a direction test, and it is the one
   place `M0.4`'s two-arm rule can be supplemented by an exact check.
2. **The transfer magnitude is large** — ±20 points on a 0–100 scale from a base of 35, i.e. more
   than half the base. `M10.A.1` requires transfer magnitude to scale **inversely** with basic
   level; 35 is near `FE03.5`'s species median, so ±20 is a mid-scale figure and the low-scale
   figure must be larger still. Useful as a sanity bound on the derivation.
3. **The observable pairing is a fixture for `M11.C.24`** — alcoholism plus housebound phobia at 15,
   responsible job plus excellent health at 55, from **identical basic levels**. Note that the
   symptom channels differ (`M1.A.11b`) while the level does not, which is the chapter's own
   demonstration that channel and amplitude are separate.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FE04.3 — ⚠ The transition at 50 is described **differently** from `M1.A.3` `[K]` `[T]` `[X]`

`M1.A.3` requires the single behavioural transition at 50 to be implemented as *a licence over joint
decisions* — below 50 the emotional system permits the intellect its own domain **except** where a
decision affects the shared life course. That derives from the papers (Ch16).

`FE04` describes the same transition as an **awareness and capacity** boundary:

> "**Above 50, the intellectual system is sufficiently developed to make a few decisions of its own.
> The intellect recognizes that a bit of discipline is needed to overrule the emotional system.**"

> "**A criterion for distinguishing people who are above rather than below 50 is that above 50 there
> is more awareness of the difference between feelings and intellectual principle.**"

*Model consequence:* **record both; do not let the later text silently overwrite the earlier.**

These are not obviously the same claim. `M1.A.3`'s form is about the **domain** over which the
intellect is licensed; `FE04.3`'s is about whether the intellect can **act at all** independently,
and about **awareness of the distinction** as the discriminator. `FE04.3` maps more naturally onto
`M4.D.1a`'s mixing weight — the self-directed channel opening — than onto a decision-scope licence.

Three reasons not to resolve this in pass 1:

- The precedence rule is *latest wins, published papers break ties on ambiguity*. `FE04` is later
  (1988 vs 1976) but Ch16 is **Bowen's own** and Chapters 1–10 here are **Kerr's**. The index's
  attribution rule forbids a Kerr formulation silently overwriting a Bowen one.
- `M1.A.3` was chosen deliberately to avoid modelling low differentiation as reduced cognitive
  capacity, which `FE02.16` independently confirms is wrong ("the intellect operates **in the
  service of** the emotional process"). `FE04.3`'s "sufficiently developed to make a few decisions
  of its own" could be misread as exactly that capacity claim.
- They may be complementary rather than competing: awareness of the difference (`FE04.3`) is the
  *precondition*, and the joint-decision domain (`M1.A.3`) is *where it bites first*.

**Carry to pass 2 as a named reconciliation item.** The likely landing is that both are kept —
`M1.A.3`'s licence as the behavioural implementation, `FE04.3`'s awareness criterion as the readout
that distinguishes the bands — but that must be argued, not assumed.

## ⭐⭐⭐ FE04.4 — ⚠ Anger is called an **unreliable guide**, against `M5.D.4` `[K]` `[T]` `[X]`

the **system's** opposition escalates to anger, which is the signal that the move landed

Read that way both stand, and `FE04.4` becomes a strong new gate:

But the reconciliation is the project's, not the source's. **Do not encode it until pass 2 has

## ⭐⭐ FE04.5 — The scale is **not an instrument** — 1988, in writing, with the reason `[K]` `[T]`

> "**The scale is primarily of theoretical importance. It was not designed as an instrument that
> could be used to assign people an exact level. It is difficult to assign an individual a specific
> level because the evaluation of one person requires a great deal of information about many
> people. Nor does the scale define clinical diagnostic categories. Rather than diagnoses, the scale
> defines an individual's adaptiveness to stress.** People at any point on the scale, **if stressed
> sufficiently, can develop physical, emotional, or social symptoms.** The higher the level of
> differentiation, however, **the more stress required to trigger a symptom.** Since the scale
> represents a continuum of adaptive functioning, **designation of any point on it as 'normal' would
> be arbitrary.**"

*Model consequence:* **Q-VALIDATION = NO, stated by the authors, in 1988, with its cause.** The
project's corpus-wide negative has until now been an *absence* observed across 22 chapters plus
`KS05.3`/`KS11.3` from 2019. Here it is asserted directly, and the reason given —
**assessing one person requires data on many** — is `M1.A.4f` exactly.

Three further consequences:

- **The scale's operational definition is `M11.C.1`.** "The scale defines an individual's
  adaptiveness to stress… the higher the level, the more stress required to trigger a symptom" *is*
  the acceptance criterion, verbatim. `M11.C.1` should quote it.
- **No immunity at the top.** Anyone can be symptomatic under enough stress, so a model in which
  high-`basic_level` agents never symptomatise is a failing implementation. Footnote 15's POW —
  "**markedly psychotic**" in the first days of captivity, recovering without treatment, "**This
  man's basic level of differentiation was very high**" — is the fixture.
- **"Normal" is arbitrary**, which `M11.F` should carry alongside `M10.C.2b`.

## ⭐⭐ FE04.6 — Basic and functional, defined in one line each `[K]` `[T]`

> "**Basic differentiation is functioning that is not dependent on the relationship process.
> Functional differentiation is functioning that is dependent on the relationship process.**"

> "**The higher the basic level, the more consistently high the functional level, and the less the
> discrepancy in functional levels of people who are closely involved with one another.** … **The
> lower the basic level, the more the functional level will fluctuate widely and the more there will
> be significant discrepancies between the functional levels of closely involved people (seesaw
> effect).**"

*Model consequence:* the cleanest definitions in the corpus, and they should replace the spec's
prose at `M1.A.5`/`M1.A.5a`. The second quote carries **two** requirements where the spec has one:

| Requirement | Spec | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `functional_level` **variance** decreases with `basic_level` | `M1.A.5a` | held |
| **cross-person discrepancy** decreases with `basic_level` | — | **not stated** |

Both follow from one mechanism — `M6.I.4`'s transfer magnitude scaling inversely with level
(`M10.A.1`) — so this is a **derived consequence to assert**, not a new mechanism to build. It is
also directly testable: three arms at low/mid/high matched `basic_level`, assert the *spread* of
functional levels within each family orders correctly. That is `M11.C.6(b)`'s variance requirement
applied within a generation rather than across them.

## ⭐⭐ FE04.7 — The estimator, and the **borrowing detector**, both stated `[K]` `[T]`

> "**Basic level is best assessed by evaluating both a person's average level of functioning over a
> lifetime and the average level of functioning of those closely involved with him. This must
> include people in past, present, and future generations.** This broad focus is necessary since
> functional level is not usually equivalent to basic level. **If a person is functioning on
> 'borrowed' self, it will be evident in the functioning of those around him.** If one spouse's
> functional level is 55 and the other's is 15, or if two parents' functional levels are each 50 and
> one of their children is 20, **a discrepancy between basic and functional levels is indicated.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M1.A.4a` and `M1.A.4f` in their primary source, plus a computable
detector.** The spec's estimator reads *elevated mean, low variance, sustained, across many
situations*. `FE04.7` adds the **cross-person** term: borrowing is detected as a **discrepancy
between closely-involved people's functional levels**, and it is diagnostic on its own.

That is cheaper than the spec's approach and complementary to it. `M11.C.20` currently separates
borrowed from basic by **withdrawing the supporting condition** and waiting; `FE04.7` says the
discrepancy is visible **without** withdrawal. Both should be implemented: the discrepancy as a
running readout, the withdrawal as the confirming test.

Note "past, present **and future** generations" — the estimate is not fully determined until the
next generation's functioning is observed, which is `M7.E.1`'s update seen from the estimator's
side.

## ⭐⭐ FE04.10 — Pseudo-self and solid self, defined — and solid self is **internal consistency** `[K]` `[T]`

> "**Pseudo-self refers to knowledge and beliefs acquired from others that are incorporated by the
> intellect and negotiable in relationships with others. Pseudo-self is created by emotional
> pressure and can be modified by emotional pressure.** … **Conviction is so fused with feeling that
> it becomes a cause.** When comprised of beliefs and opinions that are comforting or provide
> direction, **pseudo-self can reduce anxiety and enhance emotional and physical functioning. This
> can be so even if the beliefs conflict with facts.**"

> "In contrast to pseudo-self, **solid self is made up of firmly held convictions and beliefs which
> are formed slowly and can be changed only from within self. They are never changed by coercion or

> reasonably consistent with available factual knowledge, or at least not in conflict with it, is

*Model consequence:* **`M6.I.4`'s negotiability test and `M6.I.5`'s exemption in their primary
source** — the spec cites 1979 Tape 2, under a no-verbatim constraint. Two additions:

2. **Solid self has a computable definition: internal consistency.** "consistent within itself and

## ⭐ FE04.19 — Parents are **not the cause**; siblings differ by relationship `[K]` `[T]`

Footnote 8:

> "**Parents are not the cause of the amount of emotional separation achieved by their children.**
> The process operating between parents and a child that promotes or undermines emotional separation

And the transmission rule, in the body:

> "Parents function in ways that result in their children achieving **about the same** degree of

> of one set of parents separate emotionally to the same degree. This is because the characteristics
> of the parents' relationship with each child are not the same.** … it is possible for **one child
> to achieve a little more** emotional separation… **and another child to achieve a little less.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M7.E.1a`'s three-outcome distribution centred on the parental level, in

because they occupy **different parental triangles**; 1988 says because "the characteristics of the
parents' relationship with each child are not the same" — a dyadic account. The 2019 version is
more specific and, under the precedence rule, wins. **Record the direction: the mechanism sharpened
from dyad to triangle over thirty-one years.** `M7.E.1b` is unaffected.

Footnote 8's last sentence is `M11.F` material and belongs with `KS05.17` and `KS01.5`: **no output
may present parents as the cause.**

## FE04.21 — Therapy for the 0–25 band is done **through someone else** `[K]` `[T]`

> "Apart from a dysfunctional person connecting with a supportive relationship system, **the most

agent's `functional_level` responding to *others'* moves while `basic_level` does not move at all.
