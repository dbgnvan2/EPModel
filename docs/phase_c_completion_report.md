## 3. Why each failing criterion fails

These are reported, not fixed. The constants are frozen (`M10.B.4`), and changing a rule to make a criterion
pass would be tuning against it. Each needs an owner decision.

1. **`M11.C.3` comes from step 5's alliance reading of `TRIANGLE`.** The inside pair is the sender and its
   target; the outsider is the third member furthest from the sender's optimum. In the triad, a parent
   triangling the child makes the parent and child the inside pair and leaves the *other parent* outside. The
   criterion's "pair" is the parents and its "third" is the child, so it reads the opposite of what the physics
   does. Either the criterion's roles or step 5's reading must change. **Owner decision.**
2. **`M11.C.4`'s later limb.** The cutoff relieves at once, as expected. At the nodal event 22 weeks later, the
   family's anxiety is no higher in the cutoff arm. `M4.C.1c`'s accrual on a severed tie does not build enough
   over 22 weeks at the frozen rates to show at one nodal event.
3. **`M11.C.5`** has wide intervals (±17 on a difference of +6), and the sign changes across the sweep. The
   ladder is learned, per `M5.E.0`, and 60 weeks may not be long enough for it to form.
4. **`M11.C.16`** holds its direction with p ≈ 1e-44, but it does not converge. The precision rule asks for a
   half-width below 0.25 of the *baseline arm's* seed-to-seed sd. The entropy's baseline sd is small next to
   the spread of the paired difference, so 500 seeds do not reach it. `UNDETERMINED` does not pass (`M13.4`).
   Whether the precision rule should scale by the difference's sd is a plan-level question.
5. **`M11.C.27`'s [stable, remove one]** and **[unstable, add third]** cells go the opposite way at every
   setting. For a stable twosome, removing one member *lowers* the pair's deviation, where the criterion
   expects a rise. For an unstable twosome, adding a third *raises* it, where the criterion expects a fall.
6. **`M11.C.29`.** The third person accumulates no symptom weeks in either arm (0 in both), so the readout cannot
   move. The budget difference is the right sign but not significant.
7. **`M11.C.41`'s reactive-share limb.** At a lower level the mean acute anxiety rises, as expected and
   strongly. The *share* of automatic acts **falls**, though the mixing weight should raise it. One possible
   cause, not verified: lower level also lowers layer availability (`M4.D.3a`), which removes automatic acts
   from the legal set.
8. **`M11.C.42` and `M11.C.45` pass only near the frozen constants.** C.42 fails at 5 of 6 off-central
   settings and reverses at temperature 0.5. C.45 reverses at 3 of 6. Under plan D9 only the central setting
   gates, so both stand as passes, but they are fragile, and the sweep record flags each reversal. C.42 also
   survives the inverted learner (§2), so "relief reinforces the triangle" is not what produces its direction.

## 4. Changes made after the constants were frozen

The constants themselves were not changed after the freeze (`constants_frozen.md`, 2026-10-07).
`constants_changes.md` has no entry after the freeze. These code changes came after the freeze, and each is
reported here:

1. **Three numerical fixes to the policy** (step 14). The first ensemble runs crashed. Each fix has a unit test
   proved failing by reverting the fix:
   - the within-channel softmax subtracts the largest value before `exp()`, so large learned values stay
     finite: `test_m4d1_large_learned_values_keep_the_softmax_finite`;
   - `WITHHOLD` has no weight when no automatic act has any:
     `test_m4d1b_nothing_to_withhold_when_no_automatic_act_has_weight`;
   - the withheld act is drawn from the automatic channel's own within-channel weights, which stay defined when
     the mixing weight gives that channel no share:
     `test_m4d1b_withheld_act_is_drawn_when_the_automatic_channel_has_no_share`.

   None of these was chosen against a criterion. They change behaviour only where the old code crashed or
   divided by zero.
2. **`M11.C.25`'s readout was corrected after its mutant survived.** It first read "the female spouse is
   dominant". The arms differ only in which spouse is female, so a sex bias raises that readout in both arms
   alike, and the paired difference cannot move: the test could not fail. It now reads "the first spouse is
   dominant", which a sex bias does move when the sexes are swapped. The verdict is PASS both before and after.
   The fix makes the test able to fail, and it now fails under the sex-term mutant.
3. **The criterion roles moved to config** (`config/bowen/criterion_roles.md`). `M2.3a` forbids a person's id
   in Python source, and the criteria named Ravi, Marta and Nadia. Behaviour did not change: the verdicts were
   identical before and after.
4. `hold_gain` = 0.3 was chosen at step 8 against `M5.D.3`'s "abort is the usual first outcome". It is logged in
   `constants_changes.md` and was chosen before the freeze.

**One incident during the run.** At step 15 a policy mutation check rewrote `src/bowen/policy/policy.py` in
place for a few seconds, while a background ensemble run was importing from the same working tree. A
criterion started in that window could have run mutated code. The run was stopped and every record was
regenerated from a clean tree. `tools/mutation_record.py` mutates only temporary copies of the repository,
never the working tree.
