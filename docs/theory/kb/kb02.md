# KB02 — The Theoretical Base of Family Systems Theory

**Citation rule:** cite `KB02`. ASR source — no verbatim quotation. **This is the foundational interview**; several findings here settle questions the project has been circling.

---

## ⚠⚠ K02.1 — "Emotional" = instinct, definitively, with the range stated

Confirms and extends `KB13` · K13.1, at length:

> "I would see **feelings as a superficial awareness of the emotional process**. So if you conceive of the emotional process as **equivalent to instincts** — to the forces that move animal forms in one direction or another, to the forces that result in **birds migrating**… the **salmon** finding his way back to the place where he was spawned… **On a cellular level**, the force that guides an **amoeba toward a morsel of food**, whether it be biochemical or whatever — for my frame of reference for this theory, **I would call it emotional**."

And the three-way split: "emotional is distinguished from another part of man, which is derived from his **intellect**, and then out of the combination of these is the **environment**, he responds to that too."

He also replaced *mental illness* with *emotional illness* deliberately, because "mental illness belongs to a way of thinking that sees the dysfunction as a product of the brain."

*Model consequence:* two-source and unambiguous. **The model's core quantity is instinctual, not affective; felt emotion is a surface awareness of it.** Revision 2 must define this at first use. The three-way split — instinct / intellect / environment — is also a cleaner decomposition than the model currently carries.

## ⚠ K02.2 — The theory is built on ACTIONS, not on what people say — this validates the event model

From about 1956 the project worked toward "**completely ignoring what was said**, focused on the **how, when, where, and what** of human behavior **that could be validated**." The reason: "**nobody knows why** it happened. If you ask the family, they'll give you a dozen different whys" — and "over the years, every few years, **I changed to a different why**."

It produced a method and a paper: Dysinger's 1957 *The Action Dialogue in an Intense Relationship*, which "**wiped out what was said**" and plotted only the comings and goings, the coming together, anger, rejection.

> "pretty much **all of the theory is based on actions and not what was said**."

*Model consequence:* **the strongest available justification for the model's architecture.** An event-driven agent model whose records are typed moves with senders, targets and timestamps — and no utterances — is not a simplification of the theory. It is the form the theory was built in. This is also the accurate replacement for the withdrawn "content is noise" argument (`L20.3`): the claim is not that content carries no signal, it is that **the theory was derived from action sequences and validated on them.**

## ⚠ K02.3 — Bowen states his own epistemic standard, and it explains Q-VALIDATION

> "I need only to be **convinced within me** to make a decision and to move on from there, and most people wouldn't agree with that decision."
>
> Kerr: "That makes you unscientific, doesn't it?" — **"That's right."**

> "I got to the point that **I can't prove it in my lifetime**, and I'm going to devote my life to the pursuit of this idea, and **I'll let others either prove it or disprove it**."

And a contemporaneous external critique, which he accepts as "a pretty apt description": a visiting psychologist told him he was watching the human parade go by from a truck, and "**you have no baseline that can make sense to the average researcher**."

*Model consequence:* this is the best characterisation the project has of why `Q-VALIDATION = NO`. It is **not** incapacity and **not** a principled rejection of measurement — he wanted measurement (`_KERR_INTERVIEWS.md`). It is a **deliberate trade**: develop the theory now, leave proof to others. Validation item **A6 should be restated in these terms**, because "there is no validation in the corpus" and "the author chose to defer validation" are different claims and only the second is his.

## K02.4 — Analogy is not an extension of theory — third statement

> "the degree to which people from Georgetown have been able to make **analogies** with subhuman behavior. Now **an analogy is not an extension of theory.**"

On sociobiology, having observed animal behaviours identical to human ones: "then they did a **quantum jump, which I would never have done.** They say, this thing that we observe in non-humans **is the same as** humans."

The precise formulation: "What goes on in the chimps is a **definite, definable system**, and what goes on in the human is a definite, definable system, and these have **some common denominators and some differences**."

*Model consequence:* with `KB10` · K10.8 and `KB13` · K13.4 this is **three-source**. Systems at different levels share principles and are not the same system; reasoning across levels is analogy. The proposal's fractal justification must be replaced with this formulation, which is his.

## K02.5 — Bound anxiety leaves the system while it is bound `[T]`

> "There are people out there who look **cool as cucumbers**, but whose anxiety is **bound in some personality characteristic**, which is not recognized… **as long as they're bound, they are not a functional part of the family system.** To a little small degree, but not too much."

*Model consequence:* completes `KB04` · K04.5. Bound anxiety is **removed from circulation** — it must not participate in the routing budget while bound, and it returns when the binder is disturbed. It is also a fourth readout that lies: apparent calm can be binding, not differentiation.

## K02.6 — The vocabulary was chosen for cross-disciplinary legibility, not precision `[T]`

> "the term **differentiation**… is a common term in **biology**, and I thought with that term, **biologists might be able to hear**, and social scientists might be able to think toward biology."

He adds that he has "been sort of unhappy with the term."

*Model consequence:* explains the pattern of terminological regret across the corpus — *triangle* as "my most unfortunate term", *undifferentiated family ego mass*, *emotional* being misread. **The terms were chosen as bridges to other disciplines, not as precise labels**, which is a standing warning against reading precision into any of them.

## K02.7 — The founding hunch, and that it is a hunch `[T]`

> "I will **bet you** that it eventually proves to be a fact that emotional illness is **similar to emotional process in all forms of life**."

Stated as "acting purely on a **guess, a hunch**", set up "way out there" to guide the work, on the reasoning that "any way you think is okay as long as it's consistent."

*Model consequence:* the foundational premise of the whole theory is offered as an organising conjecture, not a finding. Grade accordingly wherever the model leans on cross-species continuity.

## K02.8 — The observer problem at the origin `[T]`

Trained observers watching the same families "saw the family as a **group of individuals and each one of them as having a pathology**." The remedy was a de-labelling rule: everyone writing reports — nurses, aides, anyone — had to **stop using** *schizophrenia, depressed, hysterical, obsessive, patient*, because individual-theory vocabulary prevents seeing the unit.

*Model consequence:* an inter-rater problem at the foundation, reported honestly by Bowen, and it bears on A6. It also cautions the model's own use of labelled symptom channels.

## ⚠ K02.9 — One claim that must never be implemented `[X]`

A wife who "goes to bed thinking nasty things about her husband", says nothing, and the husband "maybe even… dreaming about a wife who doesn't love him" — "something got communicated, and **it's not communicated by visual, auditory, any kind of communication by the senses**."

*Recorded so it is not quietly adopted.* This is an unsupported claim of non-sensory transmission, offered anecdotally. The model has a **witness path** and a **belief layer**, both of which require an ordinary channel. **No sender-to-receiver influence may be implemented without a route** (`M1.F.3`). Adjacent to `M12`'s must-not-build list.
