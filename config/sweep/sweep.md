# EPModel version 2 — the constant sweep's rules and ranges (stage 2, `M17.E.1`)

> What `tools/version_2_sweep.py` reads: the sweep's own rules and, for each swept criterion, the `[I]` constants its
> mechanism reads with the range each is sampled over. **The engine never reads this file.** It is outside
> `config/bowen/` because the engine hash covers every `*.md` there (`tools/ensemble_record.py::code_hash`), and a
> sweep's ranges are an input to a measurement of the model, not to the model
> (`docs/implementation_plan_version_2.md` §2.1 item 4, D12). Parsed strictly: an unknown constant, a range that does
> not bracket the constant's central value in `config/bowen/constants.md`, a range outside its declared bound, a
> scale other than `log`, `linear` or `integer`, or a row without a grade raises.
>
> Every value here is `[I]`: a modelling or measurement decision with no source (`M0.2`). Each range is half to
> double the central value, the rule the Phase C plan's D9 sweep used, bounded where the constant has an admissible
> bound: a share or weight is capped at 1, the value at which what it scales is not discounted; a count of ticks is
> rounded to whole ticks and never below 1; and a range's low end is raised where half the central value could put a
> joint sample outside the engine's parameter invariants (`anxiety_band_high`'s low end is the top of
> `anxiety_band_low`'s range, since the engine requires low ≤ high), the row's last column saying so. A `log` range is
> sampled log-uniformly, so the central value is its midpoint unless the range is capped or raised (then the cap cuts
> the upper half short, or the raised low end the lower half); an `integer` range uniformly over its whole values.
>
> **Grades.** The grade column gives the constant's grade as `docs/model_explainer.md` gives it, found by grep of the
> constant's name. Of the 31 constants below (121 rows, 14 criteria), one, `defence_threshold`, is named there (§11.3,
> graded `[I]`); none of the other 30 is named there by its code name (checked 2026-10-23), so each is `[I]` until proven
> otherwise, as `config/bowen/constants.md` already grades it; the column says so. Every constant in
> `config/bowen/constants.md` is graded `[I]` there (checked 2026-10-23), so no constant a mechanism reads is left out of
> its set for carrying another grade.
>
> **Which constants a criterion's set holds** (D10): the `[I]` constants registered under the spec IDs its criterion row
> and its `M11.5` class row credit as the mechanism, the constants of the gating readout's own mechanism where the
> readout is a derived state (a symptom's onset and end, `M7.D.1`), and the learner's seven (`M4.D.6`, `M4.D.6b`,
> `M4.D.6e`, `M4.D.1`, `M4.G.3`) where the row names learning or the readout counts selected acts. The learner is left
> out where the readout is taken one week after a scripted act (`M11.C.27`) or the row credits no learned mechanism
> (`M11.C.29`: the budget limb and the third's symptom time course; `M11.C.41`: the mixing weight and the stress
> response), and a constant that never acts in the criterion's scenario is left out
> (`nodal_spike_intensity`: no nodal event; `functional_level_floor`: a numerical guard). Each row's last column names
> the spec ID and says what the constant does in that criterion. These are judgments; the plan's §2.10 lists them.
>
> Decided as recommended under the owner's instruction of 2026-10-22, pending the owner's review
> (`docs/implementation_plan_version_2.md` §2.2, D8, D10, D12; §2.10, the Phase C sweep).

## Rules

| constant | value | grade | unit | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sweep_samples` | 8 | [I] | samples per criterion | the pilot's count (D10): enough to time the runs, not a measurement of a criterion |
| `sweep_seed` | 2 | [I] | generator seed | with the criterion's key, seeds the draw of its samples; a longer draw begins with the shorter one |
| `detection_limit_multiple` | 2.0 | [I] | half-widths | D8 (`M17.A.1`): a first run whose gating effect is within this many half-widths of zero is run again |
| `detection_limit_seeds` | 200 | [I] | seeds | D8: the fixed seed count of that second run, taken as one block; the count the owner chose for `M11.C.10` |
