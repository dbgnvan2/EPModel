# KB10 — Emotional Process in Society

**Citation rule:** cite `KB10`. ASR source — no verbatim quotation.

---

## ⚠ K10.1 — Bowen names THREE variables for societal process, and they are not our three dials

> "thinking about societal issues and systems involves… **one, a different way of thinking** — the world is different depending on the way the head is that observes it… Another… is **differentiation of self**… and a third is the **intensity of anxiety**. Those are **three main variables**. **The higher the anxiety, the greater these products, which is societal problems, regression.**"

*Model consequence:* the spec's dials are `societal_leadership`, `media_amplification`, `resource_pressure`. **These are not Bowen's three.** His are *mode of thinking*, *differentiation*, and *anxiety intensity*.

They are probably compatible rather than competing — ours are **drivers of anxiety**, which is his third variable, so they sit one level below his decomposition rather than replacing it. But the spec currently presents three dials as *the* societal model, and that is not what the source says. **Flagged for pass 2 reconciliation; do not resolve here.**

## K10.2 — All three dials are grounded, and the third one is grounded twice

**`resource_pressure`** — asked what could have triggered the regression, and explicitly setting aside "superficial thinking" like blaming the bomb: "**population increasing and disappearance of raw materials** necessary to sustain man's way of living on earth… he will perish from lack of raw materials in the midst of his own pollution."

And the mechanism, which is exactly what `M1.D.7c` needs: "these things would **not be causes**… **if man was calmer, he could adapt to and find solutions to these things.**" So resource pressure is **a driver of anxiety, not a direct cause of regression** — the anxiety produces the regression.

**`societal_leadership`** — "if the leader… when one person who is **in charge is more sure of self**, it can be a **quieter group**… It can take people who are reasonably undifferentiated and put them in a work system where the boss is sure of self and they can be comfortable there. **They didn't change, but they're in a calmer, more contained unit.**"

*Model consequence:* the dial was graded `[I]`. It is grounded, **and the mechanism is specified**: a sure-of-self leader raises the group's **functional** level without touching anyone's basic level. That is `pseudo-self` — "so that's one level of pseudo-self" — and it makes the dial act on `functional_level`, never on `basic_level`.

**`media_amplification`** — grounded separately in `KB04` · K04.9 (Three Mile Island).

## K10.3 — Pseudo-self is drawn from prevailing societal belief, and the loop is positive `[T]`

Asked to define beliefs, "a high percentage will reach out to **societal sayings, beliefs, like the Ten Commandments**… So they **hitch on to a pseudo-self**."

The loop, put by Kerr and confirmed: the pseudo-self of everyone in society "is vulnerable to drawing its ideas from prevailing social ideas, and the more this shifts based on a regression, the more people adopt these attitudes **which in turn supports the regression** — a **vicious cycle**."

*Model consequence:* **this is the societal→family coupling mechanism the model has been missing.** The dials do not act on the family directly; society supplies the *content of pseudo-self*, and pseudo-self is the conserved quantity in `M6.I.4`. It is also positive feedback, so the societal term must not be a passive input.

## K10.4 — A symptom-severity ladder ordered by differentiation `[D]`

> "A regressed or poorly differentiated family will create a certain kind of symptom… The worst ones can get into **murder**… A better integrated one will **steal cars**. A still better integrated one under pressure will **run away**."

*Model consequence:* an explicit ordering of symptom *form* by level, which the model lacks — it has three symptom channels but no severity ordering within them. Unlike Ch15's withdrawn seven-rung ladder, this one is stated as an ordering by the source. Three rungs only; do not extend it.

## K10.5 — The regression sequence, family and society in the same shape `[M]`

Parents "reach a point at which they are **unable to deal with the symptom** in the kid. They go through a period of **not being sure of themselves**, and then becoming **dogmatic, authoritative, mean, over-confident**" — and society does the same.

The institutional pathway: "when society reaches a point it is unable to deal with this, then gradually it works its way all up to the **Supreme Court**, through the higher courts, more and more people approved, until now behavior which **used to be frowned upon has become acceptable**."

And the terminal phase: "when society reaches an end point, then it **gets mad at it** and it's gonna hurt it, starts **blaming**… and in this is **war**." With the family parallel stated directly.

*Model consequence:* `L13.1`'s symptom-threshold shift, with its mechanism (institutional ratification) and its end state (blame and attack) — both of which the spec states only as a dial.

## K10.6 — Attitudes change when society has no option left `[T]`

> "Society cannot deal with it in another way in its current state. **Society no longer has an option.** If society attempted to deal with it, it would be mean and then they would get something they didn't want. So **societal attitudes change when society no longer has an option.**"

*Model consequence:* the threshold shift is not a preference change; it is capacity exhaustion. This is `KB04` · K04.13's overload claim at societal scale — the system adapts by redefining the symptom rather than by absorbing more.

## K10.7 — Over-permissiveness breeds violence, and preaching peace breeds un-peace `[T]`

> "**permissiveness can be a breeder of violence**… over-permissiveness creates violence. When the organism begins to go unpeaceful, then people begin to **preach for peace, which can create more un-peace**."

And on legislation: "cause and effect laws designed to **relieve the anxiety of the moment**, and the more we do that, **the more we promote the thing we're trying to fix**."

*Model consequence:* C1 at societal scale, corroborating `L18.4` (overleniency) and `L13.2`'s trigger — decisions taken to allay the anxiety of the moment — and adding the feedback term. It is the clearest statement in any source that **anxiety-relieving action is the thing that drives the process.**

## ⚠ K10.8 — Family-to-society is an analogy, not a logical connection

> "man and psychologists are very quick to do **simplistic analogies**… something takes place in the individual, and if something just like it takes place out here in society, therefore the individual and society are alike. **That's an analogy and not a logical connection.**"

On the triangle specifically, having said it operates in work systems and society: "it's an **analogy, but not a reasonable connection. These things don't connect up. There are some differences.**"

*Model consequence:* `agent_model_proposal.html` §7 justifies deferring the societal layer on the grounds that "Bowen is explicitly fractal… the family is the unit cell rather than a scaled-down substitute." **KB10 undercuts that justification.** The correspondence is observed, not derived, and Bowen says the levels do not connect up. The architectural decision may still be right; **the reason given for it is not his.** Must be corrected in revision 2.

## K10.9 — Method and parsimony `[T]`

> focusing on "**how, what, when and where that can be measured and validated** and tuning out the why."

> "a theory is made up of the **least number of pieces that will hang together into a story**, rather than trying to put in all of the others."

*Model consequence:* the first corroborates the framing correction in `_KERR_INTERVIEWS.md` — Bowen wanted measurement. The second is a design principle that cuts directly against a model with 60–90 free parameters, and belongs in the spec's parameter section as a stated constraint rather than an aesthetic preference.

## K10.10 — Dates `[#]`

Societal regression "dates to about **mid-1965**"; the EPA invitation that prompted the paper came in **1972**; the concept entered the theory in **1975 or 1976**. The delinquency comparison spans **1960 to 1973** — same pattern, different intensity — and covers parents, the delinquent, **and the professionals**: "counselors, courts, police are changing too, and they're changing **in the direction of regression**."

*Model consequence:* the last point is `L08.6` — the professional system is part of the emotional system — observed at societal scale over thirteen years, and it is the empirical basis for the whole societal concept.

## K10.11 — A worked case of a leader's inattention producing regression `[M]`

Bowen describes his own Family Center regressing: he "got busy with other things and wasn't paying attention", symptoms erupted over the kitchen, the supply room, the Xerox machine, and the response was to get angry at the offenders. "**The problem is in the heads of the people who run the place.**" Kerr adds that he had been "waiting for you to do something about it" — and that is named as another symptom.

*Model consequence:* the leadership office (`M1.D.4`) with **inattention** as the failure mode, not absence — the occupant is present and not attending. And the response pattern is blame, which is `M12`'s counterfeit of responsibility. A clean acceptance-criterion candidate.
