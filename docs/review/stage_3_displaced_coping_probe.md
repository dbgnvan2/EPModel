on Mac Mini1 at the same engine hash (`--variants`), and the rows were joined by seed. `M11.C.6` expects the projection
line's level to decline: treatment below baseline, a negative difference.

- **P2 alone does not move the direction.** Without F1 every variant's difference in `development` is positive,
  +0.077 to +0.127. P2 moves part of the lift from the child's own term to the parents' term, about as much as it
  takes, so the sum holds. The receiver-only forms lower `development` in both arms about equally, which shifts the
  level and not the difference.
- **Without F1, the floor holds half of the own-term lift.** In every variant without F1, all of the functioning on no
  ledger is held by a bound of the stress response ("held" equals "unledgered"). The treatment child spends more weeks
  at the stress floor than the baseline child (90.5 against 68.5 as built).
- **F1 takes that part out of the reading.** In every F1 variant the functioning on no ledger is zero; what the bounds
  held sits on the ledger, which the reading leaves out (+0.026 of the difference when shed, +0.061 to +0.065 when kept).
- **With F1 the difference is small and, with P2, not resolved.** F1 alone: +0.0288 ± 0.0245, still positive, about
  the first-order estimate. P2 `OVERFUNCTION` with F1 shed: +0.0178 ± 0.0254, an interval that includes zero; its own
  term is −0.0087 ± 0.0185 and its parents' term +0.0262 ± 0.0191. F1 kept: +0.0234 ± 0.0267; with P2,
  +0.0216 ± 0.0248, both including zero. No variant shows the decline `M11.C.6` expects. This is reported as read;
  no size was chosen to change it.
- **F1 lowers `development` in both arms**, more in the treatment arm (F1 shed: −0.070 baseline, −0.129 treatment,
  against the engine as built). That gap is the difference falling from +0.089 to +0.029.
- **Shed or kept (Q2):** the two give the same difference within their intervals, alone and with P2, so `M11.C.6` gives
  no reason to prefer either.
