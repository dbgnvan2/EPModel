# KB07 — Obstacles to Systems Thinking

**Cite `KB07`. ASR source — no verbatim quotation.**

## ⚠ K07.1 — The neutrality gauge: blaming **or praising** are both losses `[T]`

> "I don't think it is possible for the human to really apply systems thinking to human behavior until it is possible for that person to get **emotionally neutral. That is without blaming or praising.**"

His self-detectors: catching himself **diagnosing** someone — "that I would consider a loss of systems perspective" — or finding himself "**critical of somebody or praising somebody**."

*Model consequence:* a **third** coach-state detector, after `L20.2` (the urge to criticise) and `KB04` · K04.11 (head inside the patient's problem). It is the most precise of the three because it is **two-sided**: praise is as much a loss as blame. The model's coach detector is currently negative-valence only, which would miss half the cases.

## ⚠ K07.2 — Capacity to see the system is gated by one's own primary tie `[T]`

Asked what the ability to think systems tracks: "it parallels, **step by step**, any change that occurred in the relationship **with my mother**. If there was one key to unlearning, that was it." Kerr confirms the same for himself, and the generalisation is put and accepted: emotional neutrality with one's mother or principal other would mean "one would automatically have arrived at a pretty good version of systems thinking."

*Model consequence:* **coach capability is not training — it is the coach's own state in their own family.** `M1.E.2` gives the external agent a drifting reactive state; this says the *ceiling* on that state is set elsewhere, in a tie the model does not represent. It is also the mechanism behind `L17.3`'s conjoint gating.

## K07.3 — Two obstacles, and you cannot discard a theory without a replacement `[T]`

(1) **Fixedness of a way of thinking** — "you can't change a way of thinking until you have some notion of a **new way of thinking**"; it must be replaced, not merely dropped. (2) **Level of undifferentiation**, which is "the **cement, the hardener**, that fixes it" — reduce it and the thinking can change faster.

## K07.4 — A measured coaching dose, from the person coached `[#]`

Kerr, on two years of coaching at roughly six to eight contacts a year: "there were probably **four times at most, five maybe, in two years**, that something helpful came out of the contact." And what helped was not anything said — "somehow I come out of that room realising there was an **alternative way to respond**, and also that **I was emotionally caught in it**."

*Model consequence:* a dose–response figure from the recipient, and a mechanism for what a productive contact does: it makes the person aware they are caught, and that an alternative exists. Both are representable; neither is instruction.

## K07.5 — Neutrality is experienced as caring `[T]`

> "the **more neutral you get, the more they perceive you as** [caring]… It means I care, which is an emotional value."

*Model consequence:* contradicts the natural expectation that a neutral agent reads as cold. The receiver's appraisal of a neutral act is **positive**, which the model's appraisal function would get backwards if neutrality were implemented as low intensity.

## K07.6 — Polarised debate is diagnostic `[T]`

> "there is **no debate in systems**… any time anybody is stuck in a polarity, they have either not arrived at systems thinking, or **they have lost it** if they did arrive."

Corroborates `KB12` · K12.7 and gives it a sharper form: polarity is not merely correlated with low differentiation, it is evidence of a *loss* of perspective in someone who had it.
