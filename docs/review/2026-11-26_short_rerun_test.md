## Recommended mix of runs

**After each engine batch** (about 6 machine-hours; about 3.5 hours on the two machines at once, against revision 69's 18.7-hour full queue):

- The Phase D part II ensemble record and the Phase D mutation record at 1/5 of their seeds (`--seed-fraction 0.2`). They are the two slow records: at full seeds D2 took 9.5 hours of revision 69's queue and DMUT 1.8. At 1/5, D2 took about 0.3 of its full time and DMUT about 0.2. The two 600-seed `M11.C.10` rows are in it, at 120 seeds: at that count the short run caught `cut_off_tie_weeks`' change of sign. `M17.A.1`'s near-limit rerun keeps its declared 200 seeds, so a row near the detection limit gets full precision.
- Every other record at full seeds: D1, C, CMUT, SWEEP, LEVEL, DIAG, SENS, GG and T176, 2.75 hours at revision 69's times. D1 is among them, so `M11.C.12`, the one problem the short test missed, is run at full seeds under this mix. Shortening them would save under 2.2 hours and add noise to records that are cheap to run in full.
- Not the emergence audit (4.6 hours at revision 69). None of revision 66's 45 verdict moves was in it.

**Reading a short record.** A verdict that flips in a short record is a flag. It is reported as a finding when the move is large: the two effects' intervals do not overlap, so the earlier and the short effect differ by more than the sum of their half-widths. Otherwise it is borderline. A borderline move is not read as a change of the model (T-254): it is listed on the changes page, the record's header states its fraction, and its tests keep an expected-failure mark naming the mix until the milestone run.

**At a milestone only** (a stage's end, a tag, a milestone review, an outside review, the stage-end sweep): every record at full seeds and the emergence audit, about 18.7 hours at revision 69's times (D2 alone is 9.5). **Before a report quotes a number** from D2 or DMUT, that record, or the quoted rows with `--only`, is rerun at full seeds first.

**Fresh seed blocks** (`tools/t253_seed_blocks.py`, on Mac Mini1) only for a move with p between 0.01 and 0.2 that a report is about to present as an effect of the model, never after an ordinary batch.

**Where it runs** (the owner, 2026-11-26), in this order. First Mac Mini1: nice 19, up to 8 workers, any hour; it takes D2, D1 and DMUT in order. ## Conclusion

At a fifth of its seeds, revision 66's rerun caught 9 of T-264's 10 real problems when read against revision 64's full records, in about 3.7 hours instead of 13. The one it missed, `M11.C.12`'s new effect on marital conflict, was a borderline move (p 0.04 on 100 seeds) that 20 seeds cannot resolve, and the short run also flipped 4 verdicts that the full rerun did not. A short rerun of the two slow records is enough to catch a broken mechanism after each engine batch; a borderline effect needs full seeds, which belong at a milestone or before a report quotes the number.
