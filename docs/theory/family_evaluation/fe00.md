## ⭐⭐⭐ FE00.1 — The general-systems objection, argued in full, by the authors `[K]` `[T]`

**This is the primary source for `M11.F.3` / `M11.F.3a`, and it is stronger than the transcript
fragments the spec currently cites.**

> "There have also been a number of pioneering efforts to use general systems theory to integrate
> knowledge from biological, psychological, and sociological levels, but **general systems is not a
> satisfactory integrative theory. It is a kind of 'umbrella' theory that has been imposed on a
> variety of natural systems.** It does not appear that a comprehensive natural systems theory will
> be developed based on **this kind of imposition.** It seems more likely that **separate systems
> theories will be developed from the direct study of specific natural systems**, and that it will
> then be possible to integrate these separate theories into a more comprehensive one. Attempts
> toward integration using general systems ideas seem to be a matter of **putting the cart before
> the horse.**"

> "Family systems theory is important because it was **derived from the direct study of one type of
> natural system, the human family. It was not built on analogies, nor was it derived from general
> systems theory.**"

And the prohibition on analogy, stated as a methodological rule rather than a preference:

> "The problem with these attempts is that most have been based on **drawing analogies between the
> organization of living things and systems models drawn from the physical sciences. Analogies are
> not a sound basis on which to build a theory.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M11.F.3a` was recorded as a `[user]` addition (`U12`) with Kerr-2019 framing
in support. It is `[T]` in the primary source, and the primary source is this book's own opening
argument.** The grade rises from `[user]` to `[user→T]`, and the citation should be `FE00.1`
rather than a 2019 paraphrase.

**But note what it costs.** The same passage sharpens the objection against the model rather than
softening it. Kerr's rule is *imposition is the error*: a systems model is legitimate when
**derived from direct study of the specific natural system**, and illegitimate when a general
framework is **imposed** on it. The honest position for this project is therefore narrower than
`M11.F.3a` currently states — the simulation is legitimate **to the extent that every mechanism in
it is derived from observation recorded in the corpus**, and every `[I]` constant is an imposition
by exactly Kerr's definition. That is an argument for the grading discipline, not a defence of the
model as a whole. **`M11.F.3a` MUST be amended to say so.**

Note also the tension with `M11.F.2`, which requires family→society claims to be stated as
**analogy**. Kerr says analogies "are not a sound basis on which to build a theory". The two are
compatible — `M11.F.2` uses analogy to *bound* a claim, not to found one — but the wording should
not read as though analogy were being offered as support.

## ⭐⭐ FE00.2 — The phenomenon the theory exists to explain: **outcome variance within a diagnosis** `[K]` `[T]`

> "Medicine has reached a high level of sophistication in terms of its ability to describe and
> categorize a myriad of clinical entities… **What is not explained, however, is the wide range of
> clinical outcomes that are possible with almost every diagnosis.**"

Three worked instances, all with the diagnosis held constant:

> "There are patients… who suffer a serious attack of **multiple sclerosis** when they are in their
> early twenties, but who do not have another symptom the rest of their lives. Other multiple
> sclerosis patients may be seriously disabled within five years… patients diagnosed as having
> **bipolar affective disorder** who have two psychotic episodes in an entire lifetime, and others
> who have five hospitalizations in less than five years. Two people may both carry the gene
> involved in the development of **Huntington's chorea**, but one may be seriously handicapped at
> age 40 and the other exhibit only mild manifestations of the condition at age 80."

> "Notions like '**lowered resistance**' and '**stress**' are sometimes invoked to explain clinical
> variation, but such notions are **too poorly defined to be of much value.**"

*Model consequence:* **this is the explanandum, and it is a variance claim, not a mean claim.** The
model's output is being asked to reproduce *spread at fixed constitution*, which is exactly the
shape of `M11.C.6(b)`'s variance requirement and of `M1.A.11b`'s split — **constitution sets the
channel, level sets the amplitude**. `FE00.2` is the corpus's clearest statement that the target
quantity is the *variance*, and it should be quoted wherever the model's purpose is stated.

It also **retrospectively justifies `M1.A.11b`**: Huntington's carriers diverging by forty years is
a case where the constitutional factor is fixed *and known*, so everything left is the other term.

## ⭐⭐ FE00.3 — The disease claim, bounded by its own authors — narrower than the 2019 book `[K]` `[T]`

> "one assumes not only that the activity of a cancer has an impact on the psychological state of
> the patient… but also that the family relationships and psychological state have some impact on
> the activity of the cancer. **This is not to say that what is occurring in the family causes the
> cancer to act in certain ways, but only that the biological process of the cancer and the
> emotional process of the family are, to some degree, mutually influencing one another.** While
> this kind of assumption is supported by ever increasing amounts of evidence… **it still cannot be
> regarded as a proven fact.**"

*Model consequence:* **`M11.F.4`'s prohibition stands and now has support from the authors
themselves at the earlier date.** Two things are stated here that the 2019 material does not:
the relation is **mutual influence, not causation**, and it is explicitly **not a proven fact**.

This is also the project's **first measured divergence between Kerr 1988 and Kerr 2019** — the
2019 book's *unidisease* chapter (`KS23`, graded `[K-ext]` throughout) goes considerably further
than this. Record the direction: **the claim widened over thirty-one years, and the hedge
weakened.** Under the precedence rule 2019 wins on revision, but `M11.F.4` is a prohibition on
*implementing* a disease mechanism, and both texts support it.

## ⭐ FE00.4 — Content versus process, defined `[K]` `[T]`

> "theory enables a therapist to **distinguish between content and process** in evaluating a
> clinical family. **Content refers to all the various pieces of information; process refers to the
> way these pieces of information are related.** In a family, these pieces of information are
> related by virtue of the family emotional process."

*Model consequence:* a clean definition to attach to `M12.1`'s withdrawn **"content is noise"**
item. Kerr does **not** say content is noise; he says content and process are different *levels of
description* and that theory is what relates them. The withdrawn mechanism stays withdrawn, and
this is the sentence that explains why it was wrong rather than merely unsupported.

## ⭐ FE00.5 — Assessment is **continuous and revisable**, not an initialisation step `[K]` `[T]`

> "**Family assessment is not done at the beginning of therapy and assumed to be complete.
> Assessments are not written in stone, but are continually modified as new information comes to
> light.**"

*Model consequence:* supports `M1.A.4a`'s estimator being a **running** computation over a moving
window rather than a value set at `t0`, and supports `M1.A.14b`'s general rule that positions are
computed, not labelled. Also bears on `M1.E`: the external agent's model of the family is a
**belief** (`M9.1`) that updates, and `M9.5` already requires ground truth and belief to be
reportable separately.

## ⭐ FE00.6 — The therapist's failure mode is **being pulled into the family's perspective** `[K]` `[T]`

> "A family usually comes to therapy with **a fairly narrow perspective on the nature of the
> problem.** It is the therapist's responsibility **not to get pulled into the emotional whirlpool
> of the family and into this narrow perspective**, but to be able to **back up, achieve a
> reasonably broad perspective, and make decisions accordingly.**"

And the same failure stated for the clinician generally:

> "**The clinician sees only as far as his concepts for understanding problems permit him to see.**"

*Model consequence:* this is `M1.E.2`'s drifting reactive state and `M1.A.18`'s
`systems_perspective` **described as one thing rather than two** — the coach's loss of perspective
*is* capture by the family's frame. Consistent with `M1.A.18a`'s two-sided readout and with
`M4.C.4`'s decay of perspective under anxiety. Nothing new to build; a second witness that the
coach's perspective and a member's are the same variable on the same scale, which is why
`M1.A.18a` is described as `M1.E.2`'s detector "pointed at a `MEMBER`".

## FE00.7 — Assessment breadth is stated as the determinant of outcome `[K]` `[D]`

> "**Effective therapy depends on assessment; if that assessment is too narrow in scope, the therapy
> will probably be ineffective.**"

Three worked failures, each a case of the assessment excluding the operative level: a diuretic
prescribed repeatedly for leg edema in unrecognised chronic heart failure; a schizophrenic patient
hospitalised and rehospitalised without the parent–patient relationship being weighed; and — the
one that is a model claim rather than a medical one —

> "A family therapist may treat two parents and their schizophrenic son, but not attach importance
> to the fact that **the parents are emotionally cut off from their families of origin. The
> parents' cut off from the past undermines their ability to stop focusing on their son's
> problems**; once again, the therapy will be ineffective."

*Model consequence:* **direct support for `M1.D.8`** — the family must not be the boundary of the
simulated system, because cutoff with the families of origin is an *input*. Here it is stated as a
**causal chain with a named consequence**: parental cutoff → reduced capacity to withdraw focus
from the child → projection sustained. That is `M1.D.8` + `M7.E.3` + the projection path, and it is
a claim the model can be asked to reproduce. `M2.3`'s prohibition on a closed nuclear four is the
implementation of exactly this sentence.
