# KB12 — Background Aspects of Differentiation

**Citation rule:** cite `KB12`, not a speaker. ASR source — no verbatim quotation. See `_SOURCE_QUALITY.md`.

---

## K12.1 — Only one person can work on individuality at a time `[T]` — a hard sequencing constraint

> "in most families, if individuality can proceed, it proceeds, **only one person can work on individuality at a time**." One moves, the other opposes; if the mover holds ground the conflict goes away; then **after a period of calm** the other moves and the process repeats. "So it goes **step, one step at a time**."

*Model consequence:* the model permits any agent to select `I-POSITION` on any tick. This says a dyad supports **one active differentiating move at a time**, with a calm interval before the partner's turn. That is a new gate for `M5.C` and it makes the alternation in Ch10 (`L10.6`) a rule rather than an observation.

## ⚠ K12.2 — Introducing an opposing party mid-move can end it permanently `[T]` — this extends R2

A father with a schizophrenic daughter was doing "very well" at taking a stand against the mother–daughter togetherness. The mother came to the office and asked to be included — *I am important to this family too* — which the interview calls "**Mrs. Togetherness speaking out against the individuality**." The request was granted.

> "that was 20 years ago and I have tried to **rekindle the individuality in that father and I have never done it in 20 years. He lost his ability to do it.**"

The rule drawn from it: "when you have somebody moving toward individuality, you **never, never, never interfere with it**… do not bring in the contrary people who are going to put in the opposite viewpoint."

*Model consequence:* `_RESOLUTIONS.md` R2 settled that **alignment**, not knowledge, is the gate — an ally opens a peripheral triangle. **KB12 adds a second and worse failure: admitting a togetherness-agent into an active move.** The damage here is not a failed attempt but a permanent loss of capacity, which nothing currently in the model can represent. The `M8.6` alignment predicate needs a third case: `OPPOSED_TO_MOVER`, admitted mid-move, with a durable penalty.

## K12.3 — A low-level self can hold an I-position, and the manner gives it away `[T]`

> "the **low-level self can have an I-position**. That is a selfish, dogmatic, forceful kind of an I-position"… "a high-level differentiated self is **neither offensive nor defensive** to the other. So you can tell pretty much the **level of functioning of an I-position from the way they do it**."

The low-level form is a threat — if you don't change, there are consequences *for me*; the high-level form has the group in view.

*Model consequence:* **C10 with an observable.** The model has `outside_ness` as a hidden multiplier with no readout. This gives one: an I-position delivered offensively or defensively is low-level. It corroborates Ch10's fifth band (`L10.12` — dogmatic, compliant or rebellious) precisely, and it is the discriminator `M5.F.3` asks for.

## K12.4 — Brick-throwing as the landing diagnostic, with its limit `[T]`

> "when I made a solid move, the opposition would be throwing bricks. So I would use the **brick throwing as an indication that I'd made a differentiating move**, which is **accurate within certain limits**."

And the abort mechanism: "when you're trying to make a differentiating move and you **get mad** at the opposition for throwing bricks… now you're heard by the opposite side as caught up in the emotionality, **which neutralizes your effort to differentiate**."

*Model consequence:* Ch21's rule — absence of reaction means the move did not land — confirmed from a different setting, **with a hedge the model should carry** ("within certain limits"). And anger is confirmed as the gate (`M5.D.4`) with its mechanism: it changes how the move is *heard*, not what was done.

## K12.5 — Bowen's own late differentiating exercise, and it is not a weekend `[T]`

A vacation visiting both extended families, planned as a differentiating exercise between himself and his wife:

- "I literally worked **weeks** on that"
- "**I didn't say nothing to nobody about it**, just did it… **If you tell somebody you're going to do it, it doesn't come off.** You cook it up in your own head and you do it and then you find out whether it worked."
- He deliberately did **not** help his wife with her family: "I assumed she could handle herself **better than if I helped her**. It would have been a mess."
- Outcome: she "did beautifully"; his wife and sister-in-law, negative for forty years, reconciled over two days.
- "**if one person can stay out of it in a family situation, the family situation will handle itself.**"

*Model consequence:* three things at once. The **secrecy rule confirmed independently** (`L21.2`) with the same reason — telling anyone kills it. The **not-helping rule** (K12.2) confirmed from the positive side. And it corroborates `M5.D.7a` — weeks of preparation for one visit, and the payoff is in *others'* relationships, not in a score for the mover.

## K12.6 — Working the feeling channel enlarges it; working the intellect orders it `[M]`

> "if you focus on feelings, it's almost as if you are **uncovering a volcano**, and the more you uncover it, the more of this coming and coming… the only time you get a kind of resolution is when the **feelings run down**, when the volcano gets tired." Whereas "if you can work on the intellect, then the feeling world is no longer a volcano. It is an **orderly little fountain**" — and the person will attend to the feelings themselves.

*Model consequence:* a mechanism-level statement of why an intervention aimed at the emotional channel is self-defeating. It generalises C1 beyond symptom relief: **attending to a channel amplifies it.** This is also the therapeutic form of the anxiety-first ordering in `KB04` · K04.4.

## K12.7 — Polarities as a differentiation readout, at every scale `[D]`

> "The **higher the level of functioning, the more an individual can get beyond polarities**, and the lower the functioning, the more they get caught up in them."

Named polarities: right/wrong, good/bad, Democrat/Republican, men/women. "The more you set the Democrats and Republicans debating… the volcano gets bigger."

Applied to society through a Vietnam example: a leader who reacts to the most radical element of an opposition alienates its reasoned element and worsens the polarity.

*Model consequence:* a second readout that does not require estimating differentiation directly, and it scales from person to society — which is what the societal dials need. Bears on `media_amplification` (`KB04` · K04.9) and on `M1.D.7d`.

## K12.8 — Intellectual communication is heard subjectively `[T]`

> "most people **hear the intellectual viewpoint through the emotional screen**, and the answer is different than the teacher intended"… "There is something in the human brain that causes the human to **mishear** intellectual communication… he cannot hear a pure factual presentation **without rearranging it**."

*Model consequence:* the belief layer (`M9`) and the anxiety gain on appraisal (`M4.C.2`) are the same mechanism seen from two sides, and this states it directly. It also predicts that the *receiver's* rearrangement is systematic, not noise — which `M9.2` requires and does not yet specify.

## K12.9 — The differentiation model is a team, not a solitary act `[M]`

Differentiation is modelled on a football team — highly developed individuals who must also play as a group — with the coach's job being "to help each player become **all they could be**, and at the same time, play as a member of the team."

*Model consequence:* individuality and togetherness are not a tension to be resolved in favour of one. The target state is high on both, which is what `M6.I.3`'s life-energy budget must not preclude — a zero-sum allocation between relationship-seeking and goal-directed activity should not make a well-differentiated agent relationally absent.

## K12.10 — Scope limit, stated plainly `[T]`

> "**schizophrenia does not have the ability to move toward differentiation**, in general that is so."

*Model consequence:* an upper bound on what the differentiating move can reach, and it is stated as a general rule with an exception acknowledged in the same breath (the father in K12.2 was the one who could).
