# KB13 — The Changing World and Family Therapy

**Citation rule:** cite `KB13`. ASR source — no verbatim quotation. Much of this interview is field history and the politics of the discipline; the findings below are the parts that bear on the model.

---

## ⚠⚠ K13.1 — "Emotional" means INSTINCT, not feeling — and the model has been reading it the wrong way

> "under this thing called feelings, there was a component of the human being which goes **all the way back to the one cell forms**. It would be the **life force that directs a one-cell form toward food or away**… There would be something that is called **instinct**. So I wanted to make that the primary thing in the theory, which I called an **emotional system, which would be synonymous with instinct**, and could include feelings."

And the correction, stated as his own fault:

> "people have picked up this term I used, **emotional, and made it synonymous with feeling, which I didn't mean at all**, but I mean something **a lot deeper** than that. So I'm partially at fault with that."

Its locus: "that part of the human being which has to do with the **hindbrain and the midbrain and not the forebrain**, and this has to do with instinct." He credits Darwin's usage — an animal showing its teeth — as the sense he intends: instinctual self-defence, with feeling as a component rather than the whole.

*Model consequence, and it is large.* The spec and explainer use *emotional* throughout — emotional system, emotional process, emotional reactivity, emotional cutoff — and the model's implicit reading has been affective. **It is not affective. It is instinctual**, with feeling as a subset. Three things follow:

1. `acute_anxiety` and `chronic_anxiety` are **not** feeling-states. They are instinctual activation, of which felt emotion is one expression and which can operate without it.
2. This is why **overt emotionality is not a proxy for level** (`L16.4`) and why the withdrawn person is as involved as the effervescent one (`KB05` · K05.7) — both are equally *instinctually* engaged and differ only in the felt/expressed component.
3. It joins `KB12` · K12.6 (working the feeling channel enlarges it) and `KB15` · K15.1 (automatic versus conscious) into one claim: **the model's core quantity is sub-affective.**

**Revision 2 must decide whether to rename the model's terms or to define *emotional* explicitly at first use.** Defining it is probably enough; renaming would break every citation to the corpus.

## ⚠ K13.2 — Bowen rejects general systems theory as the foundation

> "there is one way toward bringing the disciplines together, and that is to get a way of thinking that supersedes the thinking of all the disciplines… **I don't think general systems is an answer to that**… I would see something that has to do with **natural systems**."

The distinction he draws: natural systems are rooted in the earth and in biological evolution; general systems "came out of man's head, along with mathematics and those things."

*Model consequence:* this is the accurate and much sharper version of the framing question that `L17.5` mishandled twice. Bowen is not against formalism and he wants measurement (`_KERR_INTERVIEWS.md`); what he rejects is a foundation **derived from human reasoning rather than from nature**. A simulation is a general-systems artifact by construction, so this is a real and named objection to the model's foundation — not a fatal one, but it must be stated accurately in the framing rather than softened. **Revision 2.**

## K13.3 — Theory must be rooted in nature — third independent statement `[T]`

> "theory to be worth anything, to be worthy of the name theory, it has to be **connected to the earth, science, and the universe**. It has to be connected to the **seasons, the tides, the natural phenomena**."

*Model consequence:* with `KB01` ("the force that causes grass to grow, that causes the world to turn") and `KB10` ("measure the how, what, when and where; tune out the why"), this is now **three-source**. The framing correction already made in `_KERR_INTERVIEWS.md` is confirmed and can be stated firmly.

## K13.4 — Society is like a family, but not the same as one `[T]` — the calibrated version

> "the total of society goes through ups and downs that are **similar to a family. Not the same as, but the same principles apply.**"

*Model consequence:* this is the precise form, and it is more usable than `KB10` · K10.8's flat "analogy". Together: **the same principles apply; the levels are not the same thing; and reasoning from one to the other is an analogy rather than a derivation.** The proposal's fractal justification for deferring the societal layer must be restated in these terms.

## K13.5 — Anyone can lead a group if sure enough of themselves `[T]`

> "**anybody can be in charge of any group at any time as long as they're sure enough themselves.** That's all it takes, and when anyone in a family or a larger group can be sure of themselves enough to take a stand on it, then **all of the others oppose**, and if the leader can stand there, **eventually the group will come to respect it**."

With a scale qualifier: in a family one can take a stand on almost anything as long as it is a principle; in larger groups, "the more principle can be based on **science and theory**, the more solid it is."

*Model consequence:* the change-back sequence (`M5.D`) applied to a leadership position, corroborating `L20.5` and `KB10` · K10.2. The scale qualifier is new — **what a principle must rest on varies with group size**, which the model's sphere-of-responsibility permission (`M1.D.4`) does not capture.

## K13.6 — Practitioners regress with the society they serve `[M]`

> "what is done in family therapy is **dictated by the opinions of the masses**… practitioners will change what they do in response to societal pressures… disciplines swayed by public opinion… we're in a direct line to go into a regression."

*Model consequence:* `L08.6` — the professional system is part of the emotional system — extended to the whole profession and driven by the societal dial. The model's external agents inherit the societal term; they are not a fixed reference point. This is testable and is not currently in `M1.E`.

## K13.7 — Self-assessment of the societal prediction `[X]`

> "it is working out **almost to a T, the way I predicted it back in the early '70s**."

*Recorded, not accepted.* It is the author grading his own forecast, with no stated criterion and no comparison. Note it alongside `L14.4`, where he reports a different prediction of his own as having failed — which is the reason to take this one as testimony rather than evidence.

## K13.8 — On the discipline's trajectory `[T]`

Family therapy as currently defined will be "known someday as a **misadventure**" and will "decline, perish", because it declared itself a separate discipline without maintaining connectedness to medicine and biology — while natural systems theory persists. Splintering is named as the mechanism, and the analogy drawn is to denominations in religion.

*Model consequence:* none directly. Recorded because it is the clearest statement of what Bowen thought his own contribution was and was not, and it bears on how the project should describe what it is modelling — natural systems theory, not family therapy.
