## ✂ CORRECTED FINDINGS

### Ch03 header — "positions do not yet shift" is FALSE
Pass 1's headline chronological claim propagated into the ledger header and the timeline. Membership
is fixed and nobody is swapped in or out — **but positions shift on two axes**, and Bowen states it
as his general finding: "the parent in the overadequate or strong position would **shift** to the
inadequate position." What is genuinely later is the *generalisation to any threesome*, *recruitment
of a third under tension*, and *interlocking triangles*.

### "Investment" has a definition, and pass 1 inverted its sign
Ch04 defines it in text: "the thoughts of both, **whether positive or negative**, are largely
invested in each other." It is **share of thought occupied by the target, valence-blind**, traded
across targets. **Conflict-laden preoccupation is HIGH investment.** Any implementation deriving
investment from warmth or agreement inverts the sign on exactly the families this is about.
It is also an **inference by the observers**, not self-report, not time spent, with no instrument.

### The seesaw is a CONJUNCTION, not a difference
"more invested in each other than **either** was in the patient" / "when **either** parent became
more invested…" — a **min over both parents**, and one parent alone drives the regression.
Pass 1's `investment(p→spouse) − investment(p→child)` lets a strong marriage mask a defecting parent.

### The father–child edge has two gates at two levels
Contact-level closeness is granted freely by the mother (and assigns the father the "representative
of the mother" position). **Primary** relationship position is gated by the marital state. The six
failures are attributed in-text to the **mother's non-relinquishment**, not to the emotional divorce —
which is a separate sentence with **no case evidence attached at all**.

### C1's mechanism was mis-specified (Ch06)
Pass 1 glossed it as "distress is the fuel of differentiation." The chapter says motivation is
**aimed at** symptom level: "The motivation is toward alleviation of symptoms rather than toward
changing the familiar and comfortable situation behind the symptoms." **Relief satisfies the goal
rather than depleting an energy source.** The coach's task is **retargeting the objective**, not
preserving distress — a materially different intervention. Stated three times, in three populations.

### Q-STABILIZER (L07.3) overstated
Not independent of level. High-identity spouses "generally are able to find **a wider range** of
effective stabilizing mechanisms" — level **gates the repertoire**; only the *realised course* is
decoupled. Pass 1 also recorded the benefit without the price: both of Bowen's low-identity exemplars
are **another member's impairment**. And it missed the second termination condition — the
stabilisation holds "as long as the incapacitated one **lives**", so **death destabilises exactly as
recovery does**.

### The reciprocity switching rule (Ch03), missed by pass 1
The pole flips when the inadequate one's self-assertion is **"greater than"** the other's aggression
and domination — a **relative** comparison — flips **immediately**, and holds only if the mover
sustains it through the counter-reaction. Also: the pole is held **per area of joint activity**, and
"reducing the areas of joint activity" operates on the **same variable** — pass 1 split one variable
into two (L03.1 and L03.2 are one mechanism, not two).

### The fused default (Ch07), missed entirely
Each alters self to manage the other's functioning while demanding the other change — "neither
responsible for self". This is the **term-for-term inverse of the differentiating move** and belongs
in the policy as the default state, not as an absence.
